# How varied are the neighbors of daiin?

> Plain-English reading guide to the archived snapshot. The linked reports contain the full rules, results, and qualifications. This guide is editorial material added after the original archive.

## What we tested

Compared neighboring-word diversity under matched sampling and tested a specific prediction about nearby content-word candidates.

## What we found

Neighbor diversity varied by section and hand, but none of the twelve content-prediction tests passed. Sparse comparison groups and hundreds of counterexamples were retained.

## What remains unresolved

The results do not establish daiin as a function word, content word, rebus, or phonetic spelling. Descriptive ranks are not semantic evidence.
