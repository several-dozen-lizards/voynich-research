# Are some word positions unusually spelled for a simple template?

> Plain-English reading guide to the archived snapshot. The linked reports contain the full rules, results, and qualifications. This guide is editorial material added after the original archive.

## What we tested

Learned written-character pair models on separate folios and compared word-position surprise while matching length and training frequency.

## What we found

The hand-2 botanical first/interior comparison passed in both readings, with the same direction on all seven test folios. Other comparisons failed or lacked support.

## What remains unresolved

The training sample was small and the split retrospective. Model-relative unusualness is not proof of a name, pronunciation, or phonetic encoding.
