# Sharing word identity across nearby positions

> Plain-English reading guide to the archived snapshot. The linked reports contain the full rules, results, and qualifications. This guide is editorial material added after the original archive.

## What we tested

Added shared word-identity evidence while retaining position-specific context, with a duplication control for comparison.

## What we found

The candidate made more decisions and improved observed role recovery on the supported development panels. Its probability estimates worsened, including greater confidence in some wrong answers, and the corrected comparisons did not confirm the gain independently.

## What remains unresolved

The candidate remained exploratory. Higher coverage and raw accuracy do not by themselves establish reliable confidence or generalization to new sources.
