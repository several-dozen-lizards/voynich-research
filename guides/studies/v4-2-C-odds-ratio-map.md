# Comparing association size across contexts

> Plain-English reading guide to the archived snapshot. The linked reports contain the full rules, results, and qualifications. This guide is editorial material added after the original archive.

## What we tested

Displayed descriptive odds ratios and risk differences for the existing sh/ch-to-qok event population, with available-only comparisons primary.

## What we found

Effect estimates varied across contexts, and several intervals could not be estimated reliably. The map keeps unsupported intervals and the two readings separate.

## What remains unresolved

An odds ratio is not a difference in percentage points. The map supplies no new significance test, global search correction, causal explanation, or meaning.
