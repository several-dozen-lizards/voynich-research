# Repairing the broad opening-search correction

> Plain-English reading guide to the archived snapshot. The linked reports contain the full rules, results, and qualifications. This guide is editorial material added after the original archive.

## What we tested

Qualified a separately frozen correction based on the strongest standardized count contrast in each shuffled dataset.

## What we found

The numerical correction controls passed. This addressed the earlier test's finite-tail precision problem without relabelling its old p-values or discarding unsuccessful candidates.

## What remains unresolved

This qualified a specified statistical instrument, not a language interpretation. Its scope and correction family still govern any manuscript application.
