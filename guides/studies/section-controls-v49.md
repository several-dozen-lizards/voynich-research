# Which section differences survive basic matching?

> Plain-English reading guide to the archived snapshot. The linked reports contain the full rules, results, and qualifications. This guide is editorial material added after the original archive.

## What we tested

Compared written word parts while holding writer, manuscript variety, text format, and broad word position fixed wherever support allowed.

## What we found

Several related contrasts survived in both readings, including more qo beginnings in bath text and certain ke/eol patterns in Recipes. These are overlapping families of observations rather than independent discoveries.

## What remains unresolved

Some sections could not be compared fairly because coverage did not overlap. Surviving distribution differences do not identify meanings, syllables, or grammatical categories.
