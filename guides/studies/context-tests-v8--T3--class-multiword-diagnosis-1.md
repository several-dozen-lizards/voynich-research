# Why did the spelling-class frame search find nothing?

> Plain-English reading guide to the archived snapshot. The linked reports contain the full rules, results, and qualifications. This guide is editorial material added after the original archive.

## What we tested

Audited each frozen discovery requirement against every screened early paragraph frame.

## What we found

Every manuscript frame missed the required paragraph-presence threshold; recurrence rejected nearly all of them as well. The selected Latin positive frames met the requirements.

## What remains unresolved

The audit preserved the no-candidate result and did not lower thresholds or open confirmation. The Latin transformations were one source, not independent positive corpora.
