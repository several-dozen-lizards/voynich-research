# Do repeated e signs vary differently in labels and prose?

> Plain-English reading guide to the archived snapshot. The linked reports contain the full rules, results, and qualifications. This guide is editorial material added after the original archive.

## What we tested

Compared forms that differ only in their runs of e signs, requiring comparable examples on the same page and panel.

## What we found

The required Recipes comparison lacked enough examples in both formats. The supposed three-step illustrative family also needed correction: its longest form was not attested as a fully readable token.

## What remains unresolved

Insufficient support is not evidence that the formats behave equally. A counting or numeral interpretation was not tested.
