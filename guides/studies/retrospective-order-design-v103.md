# Designing a test beyond adjacent transitions

> Plain-English reading guide to the archived snapshot. The linked reports contain the full rules, results, and qualifications. This guide is editorial material added after the original archive.

## What we tested

Specified a new conditional test for one-word-gap order while fixing each span's immediate transition counts and the full original search family.

## What we found

The design was frozen as a new candidate. It aimed to address discreteness and asymmetry in the earlier test rather than select a smaller winning search.

## What remains unresolved

Full qualification and manuscript scoring were not completed in this folder. A new statistic does not come with a promised sensitivity improvement.
