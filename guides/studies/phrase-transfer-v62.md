# Do phrases selected on some folios recur on others?

> Plain-English reading guide to the archived snapshot. The linked reports contain the full rules, results, and qualifications. This guide is editorial material added after the original archive.

## What we tested

Selected candidate pairs on training folios, froze them, and tested separate folios with a correction across the fixed family.

## What we found

One pair passed globally in one reading but was not selected by the other. No pair achieved the required global paired success across both readings.

## What remains unresolved

This used one retrospective split of already available text. Neither failure nor a one-reading success establishes a phrase's meaning or the absence of linguistic structure.
