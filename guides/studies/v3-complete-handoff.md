# A consolidated account of the v3 study sequence

> Plain-English reading guide to the archived snapshot. The linked reports contain the full rules, results, and qualifications. This guide is editorial material added after the original archive.

## What we tested

Collected existing v3.1/v3.2 reports, the completed qualification follow-up, the heterogeneity diagnosis, and later retrospective interpretation into one handoff.

## What we found

The larger fixed controls qualified the specified A/Q1 cells while preserving their earlier failures. The separate B-Q2 problem and the limits on family-level descriptive counts remained visible.

## What remains unresolved

Consolidation did not run a new calculation or create fresh manuscript evidence. Read the separately owned replacement, re-adjudication, and descriptive reports for their distinct scopes.
