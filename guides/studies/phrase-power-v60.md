# Can the same test detect phrases in readable text?

> Plain-English reading guide to the archived snapshot. The linked reports contain the full rules, results, and qualifications. This guide is editorial material added after the original archive.

## What we tested

Applied the unchanged broad phrase test to known astrolabe and herbal prose placed into manuscript-like line templates.

## What we found

Some Chaucer phrases were detected, while the herbal sample did not pass in both layouts. Detection also depended on the artificial line arrangement.

## What remains unresolved

Single samples are not a reliable power estimate. A negative manuscript result cannot distinguish ordinary herbal prose from other possibilities when this known herbal control also goes undetected.
