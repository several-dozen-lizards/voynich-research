# Pushing the decomposition sweep to its terminal settings

> Plain-English reading guide to the archived snapshot. The linked reports contain the full rules, results, and qualifications. This guide is editorial material added after the original archive.

## What we tested

Completed the registered terminal cases and assembled full ranked comparisons with earlier settings and comparator systems.

## What we found

None matched all three descriptive bands. Many matched the model-gain band while missing the other measures, showing why one appealing metric was insufficient.

## What remains unresolved

Aliases and reused endpoints are not independent replications. The bands do not establish equivalence, language identity, or a decipherment.
