# Can local context recover independently labelled roles?

> Plain-English reading guide to the archived snapshot. The linked reports contain the full rules, results, and qualifications. This guide is editorial material added after the original archive.

## What we tested

Tested automatic selection of the number of roles on constructed cases, then separately tested a fixed two-role method against external Latin annotations.

## What we found

Automatic role-count selection failed several required controls. The historical transfer produced no fully passing panel; some panels failed and others lacked enough support.

## What remains unresolved

The failures apply to this representation, method, and sample. No Voynich roles or meanings were fitted, and a supervised diagnostic was not treated as successful blind discovery.
