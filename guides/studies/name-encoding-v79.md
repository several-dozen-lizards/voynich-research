# Does removing vowels make name recovery easier?

> Plain-English reading guide to the archived snapshot. The linked reports contain the full rules, results, and qualifications. This guide is editorial material added after the original archive.

## What we tested

Tested several letter and syllable-like encodings on a small set of known botanical name labels with correct pairings supplied.

## What we found

None met the required reliability across the tested splits, although some recovered many transformed strings. Wrong-pair controls and unresolved units were preserved.

## What remains unresolved

Recovering a consonant skeleton is not recovering a full historical name. This deliberately easy synthetic task did not qualify a Voynich sound mapping.
