# The first circular and radial direction calibration

> Plain-English reading guide to the archived snapshot. The linked reports contain the full rules, results, and qualifications. This guide is editorial material added after the original archive.

## What we tested

Tested direction recovery across length bins on paragraph-derived circular and radial examples and known-text controls.

## What we found

Longer circular examples could pass, but radial direction did not qualify. The original gate sequence therefore stopped.

## What remains unresolved

A failed short-text calibration is not a discovered direction for the manuscript's radial labels. No actual ring interpretation was produced.
