# Does a noticed qo-word arrangement survive search correction?

> Plain-English reading guide to the archived snapshot. The linked reports contain the full rules, results, and qualifications. This guide is editorial material added after the original archive.

## What we tested

Compared the selected ordering of related qo-prefixed and unprefixed word pairs with two frequency- and position-aware shuffle comparisons.

## What we found

The pattern exceeded the average shuffle, especially in one reading, but did not pass the declared search correction in either reading.

## What remains unresolved

This retrospective result neither identifies a prefix's function nor demonstrates that the text lacks structure. The wider search that produced the pattern matters.
