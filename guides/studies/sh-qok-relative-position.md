# Relaxing exact position to recover comparisons

> Plain-English reading guide to the archived snapshot. The linked reports contain the full rules, results, and qualifications. This guide is editorial material added after the original archive.

## What we tested

Grouped same-body pairs by relative line position while preserving retained-line composition and follower availability.

## What we found

The original-volume constructed cases were unsupported. A larger-volume sensitivity improved support in some regimes, but the full candidate remained unqualified.

## What remains unresolved

Four times as many active lines is a different sample-size experiment, not evidence that the original material was adequate. Relaxed matching still needed nuisance checks.
