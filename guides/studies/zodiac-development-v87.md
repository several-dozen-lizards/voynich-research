# Why the first real degree-table application could not be estimated

> Plain-English reading guide to the archived snapshot. The linked reports contain the full rules, results, and qualifications. This guide is editorial material added after the original archive.

## What we tested

Applied the frozen alignment scorer to the first-pass clothing and enclosure inventory while controlling for group frequencies.

## What we found

Both stored features were constant within groups, leaving no variation to align. The numerical zero scores and p-values of one were degenerate outputs, not informative negative tests or winning rules.

## What remains unresolved

A later image audit showed that the clothing uniformity was partly an annotation artifact. This folder preserves the original application rather than rewriting it with corrected observations.
