# Paragraph specificity and reading-robustness results

> Plain-English reading guide to the archived snapshot. The linked reports contain the full rules, results, and qualifications. This guide is editorial material added after the original archive.

## What we tested

Compared the junction measure with fixed alternatives, tested alternate-reading robustness, and retained predeclared subgroup breakdowns.

## What we found

Aggregate specificity passed, but aggregate reading robustness failed. Subgroups had mixed passes, failures, and insufficient support; a later adapter diagnostic qualified how the reading comparison could be interpreted.

## What remains unresolved

The source families had earlier exposure. Read the subsequent K2D and corrected-source reports before treating numerical repeatability as source or paragraph validity.
