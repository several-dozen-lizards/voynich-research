# How much could the transition-preserving test miss?

> Plain-English reading guide to the archived snapshot. The linked reports contain the full rules, results, and qualifications. This guide is editorial material added after the original archive.

## What we tested

Measured detection on the actual mixture of manuscript-layout constraints while retaining the original broad search correction.

## What we found

Every required layout/view combination missed the frozen power benchmarks. The calculation could be exact while still having a poor chance of detecting the specified moderate alternatives.

## What remains unresolved

The earlier nonpassing gap result was not a decisive absence finding. No manuscript result was rescored or acceptance threshold relaxed.
