# Trying grouped signs as single alphabetic units

> Plain-English reading guide to the archived snapshot. The linked reports contain the full rules, results, and qualifications. This guide is editorial material added after the original archive.

## What we tested

Treated selected EVA sign combinations as single units, qualified the restricted decoder on known text, then applied frozen Latin and German keys to reserved windows.

## What we found

The known-text checks succeeded, but the inspected manuscript outputs did not yield a coherent reading. Dictionary-word hits were recorded as vocabulary overlap rather than translation accuracy.

## What remains unresolved

Only this group-to-letter scheme was tested. The result does not reject syllabic, rebus, or broader phonetic systems.
