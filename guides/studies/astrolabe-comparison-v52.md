# Comparing descriptions and instructions

> Plain-English reading guide to the archived snapshot. The linked reports contain the full rules, results, and qualifications. This guide is editorial material added after the original archive.

## What we tested

Compared repetition and word-part patterns in Chaucer's astrolabe text with selected Voynich passages.

## What we found

Chaucer repeated whole words more often, while Voynich endings were more concentrated. These are descriptive differences between the selected samples.

## What remains unresolved

Spelling systems, alphabets, edited text, and mixed passage types make direct interpretation difficult. This did not validate a way to identify instructions or translate them.
