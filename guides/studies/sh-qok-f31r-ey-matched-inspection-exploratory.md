# Inspecting an influential folio's actual counts

> Plain-English reading guide to the archived snapshot. The linked reports contain the full rules, results, and qualifications. This guide is editorial material added after the original archive.

## What we tested

Examined the ey-versus-other-ending counts on f31r after the omission analysis identified its influence.

## What we found

The strong-looking local gap depended on very small ey counts, including zero following qok events in the two sh/ey examples. Restricting to available followers reduced the comparison denominator while retaining its qok events.

## What remains unresolved

The page was selected after its influence was known. These transparent counts are not a fresh test or evidence of a general ey rule.
