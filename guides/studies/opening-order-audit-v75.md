# Does sh precede qok across manuscript contexts?

> Plain-English reading guide to the archived snapshot. The linked reports contain the full rules, results, and qualifications. This guide is editorial material added after the original archive.

## What we tested

Counted immediate written-word order separately by hand, section, variety, and reading, then compared with within-line shuffles and a broad opening-family search.

## What we found

Some raw contrasts, especially in the bath section, were strong. The original broad correction was constrained by finite-shuffle precision and did not support treating its nonpassing result as absence of order.

## What remains unresolved

This version exposed a measurement limit. The relation's meaning and mechanism were unresolved, and the two transcriptions were not independent manuscript samples.
