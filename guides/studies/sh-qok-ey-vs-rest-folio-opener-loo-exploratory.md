# Does one folio drive the ey comparison?

> Plain-English reading guide to the archived snapshot. The linked reports contain the full rules, results, and qualifications. This guide is editorial material added after the original archive.

## What we tested

Repeated the matched descriptive comparison while omitting one whole recorded folio at a time in botanical and Rosettes contexts.

## What we found

Some botanical comparisons could change sign under a single omission, while the listed Rosettes omissions retained the lower-ey direction. Zero-weight omissions were retained explicitly.

## What remains unresolved

Stability under these omissions is not independent replication or statistical significance. Small and dependent source populations still limit interpretation.
