# Is oal specific to astronomy?

> Plain-English reading guide to the archived snapshot. The linked reports contain the full rules, results, and qualifications. This guide is editorial material added after the original archive.

## What we tested

Checked claims about oal distribution and literal al/ch subdivisions against the complete available reading inventory.

## What we found

The proposed astronomy-exclusive prediction failed: oal occurs in several other sections. The broader alch count referred to substring-bearing tokens rather than standalone words, and simple proportional distribution was not established either.

## What remains unresolved

A narrower rare oalch lead remains exploratory. Hand, format, coverage, and alternative word boundaries prevent a semantic interpretation.
