# Can any local passage be certified as untouched?

> Plain-English reading guide to the archived snapshot. The linked reports contain the full rules, results, and qualifications. This guide is editorial material added after the original archive.

## What we tested

Audited source locations against saved direct-target searches, broader atlases, and exposure receipts before considering a new power study.

## What we found

No local reserve could be certified. Some text had direct target exposure; other text had uncertain freshness rather than demonstrated protection.

## What remains unresolved

Availability alone does not prove a person inspected every passage, but uncertainty cannot be relabelled untouched. No power or new order score was calculated.
