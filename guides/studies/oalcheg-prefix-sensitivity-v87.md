# Keeping competing subdivisions of oalcheg

> Plain-English reading guide to the archived snapshot. The linked reports contain the full rules, results, and qualifications. This guide is editorial material added after the original archive.

## What we tested

Searched consistently for the whole word and several possible remainders after removing short openings.

## What we found

The apparent uniqueness of the full spelling did not carry over to every possible remainder; related strings occurred at additional physical locations. Competing cuts were retained rather than choosing a preferred root.

## What remains unresolved

Shared endings do not prove common meaning or a functional prefix. The earlier negative cycle comparison did not establish what those openings do.
