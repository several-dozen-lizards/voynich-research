# Does the original frame rule work on a known historical source?

> Plain-English reading guide to the archived snapshot. The linked reports contain the full rules, results, and qualifications. This guide is editorial material added after the original archive.

## What we tested

Acquired and checked a printed Latin materia-medica block, then ran the original rule through the fixed transformations.

## What we found

All four positive discovery cells returned no candidate. The method therefore remained unqualified, despite the source's explicit early quality statements.

## What remains unresolved

The witness is a later print of a medieval work, not a diplomatic manuscript transcription. Failed controls and source corrections were retained, and confirmation stayed closed.
