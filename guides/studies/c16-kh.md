# Rebuilding a proposed substitution-decoding method

> Plain-English reading guide to the archived snapshot. The linked reports contain the full rules, results, and qualifications. This guide is editorial material added after the original archive.

## What we tested

Implemented an adaptation of a published alphagram and language-model search, testing multiple reading directions and traversal choices.

## What we found

The automatic coherence selection failed: all 200 selected primary items consisted only of unknown-word placeholders. The scoring system rewarded missing vocabulary rather than useful readable output.

## What remains unresolved

The unavailable original executable, unspecified source details, and recorded adaptations prevent an exact-replication claim. No selected output was established as a translation.
