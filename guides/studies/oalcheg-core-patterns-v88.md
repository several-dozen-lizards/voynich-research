# Does removing the opening erase unusual spelling?

> Plain-English reading guide to the archived snapshot. The linked reports contain the full rules, results, and qualifications. This guide is editorial material added after the original archive.

## What we tested

Applied several predeclared initial-removal rules uniformly to candidates and comparison words, then recomputed descriptive spelling ranks.

## What we found

The opening was not the only source of atypicality: some shorter forms remained unusual. Their ranks changed with segmentation, transcription, reference set, and sometimes very small peer groups.

## What remains unresolved

An unusual remainder is not a proven linguistic root or content word. Sparse astronomical reference material and unseen signs limited the comparison.
