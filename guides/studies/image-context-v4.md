# Registering lines and independently marking pictures

> Plain-English reading guide to the archived snapshot. The linked reports contain the full rules, results, and qualifications. This guide is editorial material added after the original archive.

## What we tested

Separated picture annotation from line geometry so that text/image relationships could eventually be tested without drawing the picture labels from the text.

## What we found

The archive preserves successive packet, registration, agreement, and completion stages. A later continuation completed missing rating pairs and passed its recorded full-population agreement checks.

## What remains unresolved

The early README's zero-rating state is historical. Agreement between annotations does not itself establish the text/image hypothesis; use the dated completion and analysis receipts.
