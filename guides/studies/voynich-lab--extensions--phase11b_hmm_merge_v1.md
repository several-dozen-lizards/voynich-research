# An incomplete attempt to merge homophonic units

> Plain-English reading guide to the archived snapshot. The linked reports contain the full rules, results, and qualifications. This guide is editorial material added after the original archive.

## What we tested

Prepared a larger hidden-state model search with multiple candidate unit counts and restart fits, separating positive recovery from specificity.

## What we found

Only a small fraction of the required branches completed. The full-length primary was incomplete, so specificity could not be interpreted as successful qualification.

## What remains unresolved

Restart fits, branches, and independent sources are different counts. No letter values, language identity, or translation followed, and manuscript scoring stayed closed.
