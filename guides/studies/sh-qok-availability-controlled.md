# Preserving whether a following word is structurally possible

> Plain-English reading guide to the archived snapshot. The linked reports contain the full rules, results, and qualifications. This guide is editorial material added after the original archive.

## What we tested

Changed the conditional comparison so prefixes could exchange only within the same follower-availability pattern.

## What we found

This removed the earlier availability-driven alarms in the specified controls. Detection of the planted positive effects remained too weak, so broad qualification still failed.

## What remains unresolved

Fixing a confound can reduce useful support. Both correct false-alarm behavior and adequate sensitivity were still needed before manuscript use.
