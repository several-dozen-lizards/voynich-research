# A richer ring-direction calibration design

> Plain-English reading guide to the archived snapshot. The linked reports contain the full rules, results, and qualifications. This guide is editorial material added after the original archive.

## What we tested

Separated word and cross-boundary unit direction, generator fingerprints, origin, pooled tests, uncertainty views, and missing-source arms.

## What we found

The associated run found strong unit-direction recovery in several paragraph bins, while word direction, pooled tests, origin alternatives, and some controls failed or lacked support.

## What remains unresolved

The chains have separate gates; one pass does not clear unrelated failures. The recorded outcomes remain calibration rather than actual ring interpretation.
