# Qualifying a broad correction under fixed local transitions

> Plain-English reading guide to the archived snapshot. The linked reports contain the full rules, results, and qualifications. This guide is editorial material added after the original archive.

## What we tested

Tested exact sampling, numerical resolution, false alarms, and planted effects while preserving the full original search family and local-transition constraints.

## What we found

Sampling and numerical checks passed, but moderate-effect power failed in every required view. The frozen sequence stopped before the later robustness banks.

## What remains unresolved

Those unrun banks are not hidden successes or failures. A correct exact calculation did not make this candidate adequately sensitive for manuscript rescoring.
