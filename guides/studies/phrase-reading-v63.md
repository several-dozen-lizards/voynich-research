# Why do phrase counts differ between transcriptions?

> Plain-English reading guide to the archived snapshot. The linked reports contain the full rules, results, and qualifications. This guide is editorial material added after the original archive.

## What we tested

Audited the exact source locations behind the selected phrase pairs without refitting or conducting new significance tests.

## What we found

Several discrepancies came from joined versus split words or small spelling differences. The audit retained every alternate reading instead of harmonizing away the disagreement.

## What remains unresolved

Parsed-text differences are not expert handwriting adjudication. The inspection did not validate the proposed semantic worksheet categories.
