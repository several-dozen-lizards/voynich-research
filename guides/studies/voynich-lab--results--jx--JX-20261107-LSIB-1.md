# A corrected adapter followed by a source-fidelity failure

> Plain-English reading guide to the archived snapshot. The linked reports contain the full rules, results, and qualifications. This guide is editorial material added after the original archive.

## What we tested

Checked the corrected adapter, then collated the selected reading against the hash-bound original archive before any boundary diagnosis.

## What we found

All fifteen adapter fixtures passed. Source fidelity failed: among 3,810 selected locations, matches coexisted with held spans, unresolved identities, and conversion differences; the downstream diagnostic did not run.

## What remains unresolved

Unresolved conversion differences do not automatically identify which representation is wrong. No new direction fit, spacing interpretation, or real-ring score followed.
