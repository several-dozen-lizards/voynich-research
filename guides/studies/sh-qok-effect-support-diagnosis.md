# Separating diluted effects from too few comparable words

> Plain-English reading guide to the archived snapshot. The linked reports contain the full rules, results, and qualifications. This guide is editorial material added after the original archive.

## What we tested

Used paired constructed layouts to distinguish effect dilution from the loss of matching support when more exact word bodies are introduced.

## What we found

The experiment showed why a nominally planted effect could become smaller at eligible followers and why more diverse bodies could leave very few comparable cases. Correcting the target effect did not automatically restore power.

## What remains unresolved

These are controlled engineering explanations of a failed instrument, not observations about the manuscript's mechanism. Infeasible constructions were retained rather than forced into the test.
