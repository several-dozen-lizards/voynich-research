# Rechecking oal and possible word-boundary seams

> Plain-English reading guide to the archived snapshot. The linked reports contain the full rules, results, and qualifications. This guide is editorial material added after the original archive.

## What we tested

Independently counted oal-containing forms and possible al/ch splits across available readings, keeping literal word boundaries explicit.

## What we found

oal occurs well beyond astronomy, including standalone examples elsewhere. Some al/ch components also appear as separate words, but exact controlled comparisons remained sparse and alternate splits were disputed.

## What remains unresolved

A reusable substring is not automatically a root or morpheme. The audit did not establish the proposed seam, a function, a sound, or a star identification.
