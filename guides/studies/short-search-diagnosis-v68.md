# Why did a simple substitution search fail on known text?

> Plain-English reading guide to the archived snapshot. The linked reports contain the full rules, results, and qualifications. This guide is editorial material added after the original archive.

## What we tested

Compared repeated searches, sample size, and word ordering using fresh positions in the same known source works.

## What we found

More search starts repaired the old control failures, and the larger samples recovered the constructed mappings. Remaining small-sample failures exposed unseen letters and, in one case, an objective favoring a wrong key.

## What remains unresolved

This did not establish independent-work transfer, semantic reading, or manuscript decoding. Better search performance should not be mistaken for a recovered language.
