# A revised context predictor with limited qualification

> Plain-English reading guide to the archived snapshot. The linked reports contain the full rules, results, and qualifications. This guide is editorial material added after the original archive.

## What we tested

Introduced training-only hierarchical backoff while keeping the main feature, support, and qualification rules fixed.

## What we found

The revision passed a limited control scope, but none of the twenty-four corrected manuscript comparisons passed. Small descriptive junction gains did not settle the mechanism.

## What remains unresolved

The retained observational-equivalence control still prevents a unique grammar-versus-template conclusion. Negative comparisons do not eliminate every contextual model.
