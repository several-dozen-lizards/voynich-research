# Does the opening-order pattern survive one intervening word?

> Plain-English reading guide to the archived snapshot. The linked reports contain the full rules, results, and qualifications. This guide is editorial material added after the original archive.

## What we tested

Extended the sh/qok order comparison to three-word spans, with one readable word between the endpoints and no crossing of gaps or lines.

## What we found

The new counters passed their constructed checks, but the broad corrected manuscript search did not establish the gap effect. The earlier adjacent-order result was preserved separately.

## What remains unresolved

Even a surviving gap pattern could arise from chained local transitions. Its failure would not prove a word-boundary mechanism or rule out grammar.
