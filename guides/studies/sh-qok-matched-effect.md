# Planting the effect in the comparisons the test actually uses

> Plain-English reading guide to the archived snapshot. The linked reports contain the full rules, results, and qualifications. This guide is editorial material added after the original archive.

## What we tested

Adjusted constructed qok probabilities so that the movable matched comparisons had the intended effect, keeping structural zeros explicit.

## What we found

The analytic target effect could be made correct where feasible, but many layouts still had too little matching support to detect it. Unsupported and infeasible cases stayed in the record.

## What remains unresolved

A correctly planted effect does not guarantee a powerful test. These synthetic layouts did not establish manuscript eligibility or a writing mechanism.
