# Are there enough secure picture pairs to test native text?

> Plain-English reading guide to the archived snapshot. The linked reports contain the full rules, results, and qualifications. This guide is editorial material added after the original archive.

## What we tested

Collated localized proposed correspondences under predeclared confidence and unresolved-alternative rules before any target scoring.

## What we found

Only nine pairs were admitted as confident, short of the required fifteen. Tentative and contested proposals were not promoted to fill the gap, so calibration and target extraction did not run.

## What remains unresolved

This is a source-support stop, not proof that no further pairs exist in the literature. A picture-pair proposal also does not establish native name-word locations.
