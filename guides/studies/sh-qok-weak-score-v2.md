# Repairing the nuisance predictor without moving the gates

> Plain-English reading guide to the archived snapshot. The linked reports contain the full rules, results, and qualifications. This guide is editorial material added after the original archive.

## What we tested

Changed only the nuisance-estimation procedure while retaining the split, interaction target, adequacy rule, and required control thresholds.

## What we found

The repair passed a limited synthetic scope, but none of the four manuscript panels qualified. Some panels were uninformative because no direction was learned, rather than demonstrating a negative effect.

## What remains unresolved

A constructed-data pass and a stored p-value of one are not themselves manuscript findings. The mechanism and broader transfer remained unresolved.
