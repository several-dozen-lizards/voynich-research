# Testing the corrected clothing observations against degree tables

> Plain-English reading guide to the archived snapshot. The linked reports contain the full rules, results, and qualifications. This guide is editorial material added after the original archive.

## What we tested

Applied the unchanged qualified scorer to the already corrected clothing observations and fixed development geometry.

## What we found

Clothing now had usable variation, but its alignment did not pass. Enclosure remained constant within groups and therefore not estimable. No qualifying rule was carried forward.

## What remains unresolved

The negative result concerns the specified development test. It does not identify clothing with the table's sex categories, and the reserved zodiac signs remained unscored.
