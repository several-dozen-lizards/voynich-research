# The first spelling-class control bank

> Plain-English reading guide to the archived snapshot. The linked reports contain the full rules, results, and qualifications. This guide is editorial material added after the original archive.

## What we tested

Replaced exact-word identities with one fixed set of fifty-two operational spelling classes and tested the full discovery/confirmation pipeline.

## What we found

The reused Latin positives passed, but ten invalid generations among the scheduled control trials blocked qualification under the frozen rule. The valid negatives were not pooled with invalid trials to claim success.

## What remains unresolved

Spelling classes are not linguistic morphology. The failed bank remains failed despite the later separate replacement.
