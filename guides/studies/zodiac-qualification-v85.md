# Transcribing historical tables and qualifying the search

> Plain-English reading guide to the archived snapshot. The linked reports contain the full rules, results, and qualifications. This guide is editorial material added after the original archive.

## What we tested

Transcribed the two printed degree-table schemes and tested the complete traversal/template search on planted signals and nuisance controls.

## What we found

The numeric rows and operative legend were checked, and the frozen synthetic suite passed. The search correction included all admitted templates and offsets rather than only a favorable match.

## What remains unresolved

Two readings of one scan are not independent textual witnesses. This did not test the manuscript or establish that the depicted figures encode the historical categories; later work addressed actual image support.
