# Completing image ratings and clarifying historical-key tests

> Plain-English reading guide to the archived snapshot. The linked reports contain the full rules, results, and qualifications. This guide is editorial material added after the original archive.

## What we tested

Completed missing image-rating pairs, resolved the original short-line rule from its owning protocol, and prepared retrospective testing rules for published decipherment keys.

## What we found

The completed rating population passed its recorded agreement checks, and line-width classification was made explicit. Published-key scoring remained unrun because the required mappings and target resources were incomplete.

## What remains unresolved

Agreement between raters does not prove what text means. A retrospective key test cannot be relabelled untouched confirmation, and the B/C bridge still required its own qualification.
