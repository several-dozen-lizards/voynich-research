# Comparing ey with other ending families

> Plain-English reading guide to the archived snapshot. The linked reports contain the full rules, results, and qualifications. This guide is editorial material added after the original archive.

## What we tested

Compared observed next-qok rates after ey with pooled edy/y/other endings, excluding eey on both sides.

## What we found

The direction varied by context: ey had a higher raw rate in bath text and lower rates in some other material. The table distinguishes this ending comparison from the earlier sh-minus-ch effect.

## What remains unresolved

The pooled comparison does not adjust for page or opener composition. It is a post-hoc description, not a causal or inferential result.
