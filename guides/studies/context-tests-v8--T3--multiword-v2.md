# Qualifying the multiword revision on controls

> Plain-English reading guide to the archived snapshot. The linked reports contain the full rules, results, and qualifications. This guide is editorial material added after the original archive.

## What we tested

Allowed a one-to-three-word variable slot between exact anchor words, with fixed discovery and transfer requirements.

## What we found

The reused Latin degree-slot positive was recovered through all four fixed transformations, and none of the 1,200 fresh native-mechanism datasets supplied a discovery candidate. This met limited control qualification.

## What remains unresolved

The four positives transform one exposed source block. The earlier single-word failure remains preserved, and manuscript confirmation was not part of this control-only run.
