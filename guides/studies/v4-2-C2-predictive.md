# Does allowing a different association in each context predict better?

> Plain-English reading guide to the archived snapshot. The linked reports contain the full rules, results, and qualifications. This guide is editorial material added after the original archive.

## What we tested

Compared a common association model with context-varying deviations learned only on training folds, keeping each physical source group together.

## What we found

The varying model predicted the held-out allocation worse in both main readings under the primary partition. Fixed sensitivities and conditional score intervals were retained.

## What remains unresolved

This predicts allocation within fixed margins, not overall qok frequency. The intervals omit training-refit variability and are not an inferential model-selection verdict.
