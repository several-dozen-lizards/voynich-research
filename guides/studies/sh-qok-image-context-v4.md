# Preparing an independently labelled image-context test

> Plain-English reading guide to the archived snapshot. The linked reports contain the full rules, results, and qualifications. This guide is editorial material added after the original archive.

## What we tested

Separated line registration and picture judgments from the already exposed text events, with withheld ambiguous panels and controlled handoff packets.

## What we found

The retained checkpoint is pending workflow, not a manuscript finding. A fresh-session eligibility probe encountered inherited project context and stopped before opening the permitted inputs.

## What remains unresolved

A fresh chat or process alone does not prove the required blinding. Registration, independent ratings, agreement, support, and qualification were still needed before the outcome join.
