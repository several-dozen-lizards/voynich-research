# Comparing sh and ch with endings and bodies matched

> Plain-English reading guide to the archived snapshot. The linked reports contain the full rules, results, and qualifications. This guide is editorial material added after the original archive.

## What we tested

Tested whether the next-word qok difference survives same-page ending-family controls and a stronger exact-body comparison.

## What we found

The bath association reproduced in both main readings under the ending comparison. The starred comparisons did not clear the declared correction, and exact-body bath results were mixed across readings.

## What remains unresolved

The stronger two-scribe grammar claim did not qualify. Spelling, junction effects, and collocations remained possible explanations, and some borderline results had simulation uncertainty.
