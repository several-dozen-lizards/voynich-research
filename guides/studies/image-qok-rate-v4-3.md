# A separate qok-rate-by-image study design

> Plain-English reading guide to the archived snapshot. The linked reports contain the full rules, results, and qualifications. This guide is editorial material added after the original archive.

## What we tested

Specified a descriptive comparison of qok frequency across image categories while adjusting for section and keeping the earlier hypotheses unchanged.

## What we found

Arithmetic and token-counting checks passed on constructed examples. The retained design required source-owner timing evidence and qualified registration before any real image/text join.

## What remains unresolved

A synthetic-only qualification is not a manuscript result. No semantic class, sound assignment, or claim-level significance follows from this design.
