# How large a difference could the repaired test detect?

> Plain-English reading guide to the archived snapshot. The linked reports contain the full rules, results, and qualifications. This guide is editorial material added after the original archive.

## What we tested

Used constructed effects on the manuscript's actual support pattern to measure the unchanged repaired test's sensitivity.

## What we found

Reliable detection generally required large differences, with thresholds depending on context and transcription. Every tested twelve-percentage-point difference fell below the required sensitivity target.

## What remains unresolved

These are conditional simulation results, not estimates of the manuscript's true differences or a universal detection limit. Some cases remained inconclusive or impossible to construct.
