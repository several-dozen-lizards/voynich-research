# A decoder that leaves unknown signs unknown

> Plain-English reading guide to the archived snapshot. The linked reports contain the full rules, results, and qualifications. This guide is editorial material added after the original archive.

## What we tested

Qualified a restricted letter-mapping search on known text, then applied frozen Latin and German keys to separate manuscript windows.

## What we found

The controls showed that unresolved symbols could be masked correctly, but inspected manuscript output did not yield a coherent reading. Dictionary matches were not counted as translation accuracy.

## What remains unresolved

This narrow search does not establish or reject phonetic or rebus writing. Unseen symbols, uncertain words, source contamination, and training restrictions remain important limits.
