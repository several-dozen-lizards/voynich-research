# Testing a proposed support rule right at its boundary

> Plain-English reading guide to the archived snapshot. The linked reports contain the full rules, results, and qualifications. This guide is editorial material added after the original archive.

## What we tested

Constructed new layouts just above the proposed outcome-relevant support threshold, including one- and two-available-follower arrangements.

## What we found

The specified false-alarm and planted-effect checks passed in this narrow boundary suite. Fully blocked comparisons were prevented from inflating the support count.

## What remains unresolved

This qualifies the rule only for the constructed arrangements tested. It is not a manuscript result or a universal guarantee at that threshold.
