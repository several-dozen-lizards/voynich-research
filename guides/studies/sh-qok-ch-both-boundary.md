# When availability creates misleading apparent effects

> Plain-English reading guide to the archived snapshot. The linked reports contain the full rules, results, and qualifications. This guide is editorial material added after the original archive.

## What we tested

Stress-tested layouts with sharply different follower availability after ch and sh, including a joint boundary case.

## What we found

The earlier reference comparison produced many nuisance-only alarms because it failed to preserve the prefix/availability relationship. The apparent outcome association could arise through availability alone.

## What remains unresolved

These were failures for a claim beyond availability, not examples of a truly association-free dataset being flagged. The finding motivated a better conditional comparison.
