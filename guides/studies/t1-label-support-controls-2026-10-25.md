# Do completed picture labels support a text comparison?

> Plain-English reading guide to the archived snapshot. The linked reports contain the full rules, results, and qualifications. This guide is editorial material added after the original archive.

## What we tested

Froze exact rater agreement as the label rule, audited readable support without joining real word outcomes, and ran controls on the actual geometry.

## What we found

All lines had two ratings, but disagreements remained unresolved rather than missing. The primary comparison had no eligible folios, and the scan failed required calibration and sensitivity checks.

## What remains unresolved

Good overall annotation agreement does not create within-context comparison support. The real word/image outcome join and protected scoring remained closed.
