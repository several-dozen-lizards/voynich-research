# Do section contrasts survive a wider word-part search?

> Plain-English reading guide to the archived snapshot. The linked reports contain the full rules, results, and qualifications. This guide is editorial material added after the original archive.

## What we tested

Expanded the controlled inventory to all observed contiguous word parts and checked selected signals across retrospective folio splits and removal of frequent training words.

## What we found

Twenty-one overlapping contrasts passed the expanded screen, and four focal families retained their direction across the recorded splits. Some earlier candidates lost significance when the search family widened.

## What remains unresolved

The splits reused exposed material. Operational spelling families do not establish linguistic roots, syllables, or meanings.
