# What else appears beside qol and cheol?

> Plain-English reading guide to the archived snapshot. The linked reports contain the full rules, results, and qualifications. This guide is editorial material added after the original archive.

## What we tested

Inventoried neighboring words, shared two-sided frames, and direction differences in related written forms, using controlled manuscript strata.

## What we found

The browser exposes frequent and sparse examples, cross-page recurrence, and cases outside the originally noticed adjacency. It assigns no new ingredient, operation, or timing categories.

## What remains unresolved

Shared neighbors and spelling relations are descriptive. They need chance controls and independent semantic support before they can identify a function or meaning.
