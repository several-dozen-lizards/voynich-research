# Reading out a failed convention-count calibration

> Plain-English reading guide to the archived snapshot. The linked reports contain the full rules, results, and qualifications. This guide is editorial material added after the original archive.

## What we tested

Re-examined the saved selected-group controls and provided a requested retrospective structured-control diagnosis.

## What we found

The operating criteria still failed, including excess rejections on independent-leaf controls. The report retained the failed calibration instead of turning a favorable pooled summary into a pass.

## What remains unresolved

No new fixed-count hypothesis test was registered. The diagnostic did not authorize a manuscript physical-structure conclusion.
