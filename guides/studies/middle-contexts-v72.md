# Do three words behave alike outside one noticed frame?

> Plain-English reading guide to the archived snapshot. The linked reports contain the full rules, results, and qualifications. This guide is editorial material added after the original archive.

## What we tested

Compared ol, qokedy, and qol after excluding the original page and every occurrence of the shared frame that motivated the question.

## What we found

The interface retains their other neighbors, directed pairs, common frames, and complete source passages, with separate denominators for each target.

## What remains unresolved

These are descriptive comparisons of written words. Shared positions do not establish shared meanings, grammar, or an independently validated semantic class.
