# A focused distant-context interaction score

> Plain-English reading guide to the archived snapshot. The linked reports contain the full rules, results, and qualifications. This guide is editorial material added after the original archive.

## What we tested

Replaced a broad prediction search with one interaction score learned on separate folios and tested under several constructed nuisance and signal regimes.

## What we found

The first focused score failed its frozen qualification. All inadequate and unsupported positives were counted as nondetections, and failed development examples were retained.

## What remains unresolved

Even a successful interaction score could not uniquely distinguish grammar from a phrase template, latent register, or contextual spelling. No meaning was assigned.
