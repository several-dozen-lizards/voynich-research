# Building a fair evaluator for proposed keys and ordered names

> Plain-English reading guide to the archived snapshot. The linked reports contain the full rules, results, and qualifications. This guide is editorial material added after the original archive.

## What we tested

Developed and revised an evaluator, trap controls, historical-source intake, name-list packets, and rules for prospective versus retrospective testing.

## What we found

The first prototype failed required scientific qualification despite passing engineering checks. The later v7.2 harness qualified on its declared scope, but the screened published keys stopped at intake without scoring. Source collations and admission amendments remain separate records.

## What remains unresolved

Initial reports are historical checkpoints, not a summary of every later attempt. A qualified failure can reject a specified implementation on its tested scope, not every possible version of a broad decipherment idea.
