# Additional direction controls and paragraph-origin tests

> Plain-English reading guide to the archived snapshot. The linked reports contain the full rules, results, and qualifications. This guide is editorial material added after the original archive.

## What we tested

Added the pinned published generator and a fixed table/grille control, then tested origin recovery using the inferred direction.

## What we found

Both added generator direction chains failed their gates. One ZL paragraph-origin bin passed at the performance threshold, while the longer ZL bin and Latin origin panels failed.

## What remains unresolved

The results constrain the tested realizations and panels, not entire mechanism classes. Paragraph-to-ring transfer remained untested.
