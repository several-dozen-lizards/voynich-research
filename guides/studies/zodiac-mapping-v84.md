# Mapping zodiac figures before comparing tables

> Plain-English reading guide to the archived snapshot. The linked reports contain the full rules, results, and qualifications. This guide is editorial material added after the original archive.

## What we tested

Recorded approximate figure and adjacent-label locations, clothing and enclosure judgments, and unresolved features before inspecting the historical degree-table pages.

## What we found

The first-pass inventory preserved uncertain figure counts, unknown pigment, approximate label ownership, and irregular group geometry. The historical table pages were then sourced without performing an alignment fit.

## What remains unresolved

Approximate locators are not precise segmentation or confirmed label ownership. Several held-out images remained unannotated, and fine visual features were not established.
