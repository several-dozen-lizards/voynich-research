# Matching the ey comparison within page and opener

> Plain-English reading guide to the archived snapshot. The linked reports contain the full rules, results, and qualifications. This guide is editorial material added after the original archive.

## What we tested

Refined the ending comparison by using only page-and-opener groups containing both ending categories.

## What we found

The matched tables show how much of each raw contrast remains after the recorded page and sh/ch composition are held fixed. Sparse groups and separate reading/view results remain visible.

## What remains unresolved

The refinement was chosen after the raw contrasts were known. It supplies descriptive adjustment rather than a new independent test or causal estimate.
