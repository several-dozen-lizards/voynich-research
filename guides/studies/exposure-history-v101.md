# Had supposedly fresh passages already entered earlier work?

> Plain-English reading guide to the archived snapshot. The linked reports contain the full rules, results, and qualifications. This guide is editorial material added after the original archive.

## What we tested

Traced saved scripts, results, and conversations to distinguish simple availability from previous feature and neighbor analysis.

## What we found

The audit found substantial earlier exposure and did not establish a protected passage reserve. It carefully separated script selection scope from proof that a person inspected every token.

## What remains unresolved

It did not prove that every remaining passage had already received the exact later direction test. It also did not create new untouched material or authorize scoring.
