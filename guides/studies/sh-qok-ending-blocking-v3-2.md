# Does the association depend on ending families?

> Plain-English reading guide to the archived snapshot. The linked reports contain the full rules, results, and qualifications. This guide is editorial material added after the original archive.

## What we tested

Made a frozen descriptive breakdown by the original ending families while auditing which family outcomes had already been examined.

## What we found

The report distinguished discovery contexts, the known origin of the idea, and additional descriptive contexts. It required each individual family to have support rather than rescuing sparse families by pooling.

## What remains unresolved

These are exposed, retrospective counts with no inferential p-values. The grouped descriptions cannot be promoted to fresh confirmation or an ending's meaning.
