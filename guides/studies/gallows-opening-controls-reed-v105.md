# Do opening-line sign preferences survive tighter matching?

> Plain-English reading guide to the archived snapshot. The linked reports contain the full rules, results, and qualifications. This guide is editorial material added after the original archive.

## What we tested

Compared plume-class signs in otherwise identical word frames while matching page, position, writer, and additional layout conditions.

## What we found

The descriptive opening-line preference survived stricter matching, with p providing most of the support. Matching examples for f existed, but the reused test subset was too sparse for a firm f-specific rule.

## What remains unresolved

This is retrospective consistency on exposed pages. It does not explain a ring's start, establish a mechanism, or provide untouched confirmation.
