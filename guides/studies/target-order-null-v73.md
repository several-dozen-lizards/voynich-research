# Does qokedy precede ol and qol more than the controls predict?

> Plain-English reading guide to the archived snapshot. The linked reports contain the full rules, results, and qualifications. This guide is editorial material added after the original archive.

## What we tested

Tested the proposed directional ordering with a primary exact-position method and a separately retained within-line sensitivity.

## What we found

The primary method failed its planted-effect power gate and was not run on the manuscript. The permitted sensitivity supplied only a limited F-reading result, without the primary comparison.

## What remains unresolved

A one-reading sensitivity cannot replace the failed primary test or identify syntax. Low control sensitivity is not evidence that manuscript ordering is absent.
