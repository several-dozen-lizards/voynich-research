# Checking a proposed star-and-herb correspondence chain

> Plain-English reading guide to the archived snapshot. The linked reports contain the full rules, results, and qualifications. This guide is editorial material added after the original archive.

## What we tested

Audited the manuscript spellings and historical correspondences used to connect an oalch lead with astronomical and herbal traditions.

## What we found

Some written forms and historical associations could be sourced, but their manuscript locations did not establish a common referent. A connector label, a figure label, and paragraph text could not simply be treated as the same named star.

## What remains unresolved

The botanical identification and the link from historical tradition to Voynich words remained unsupported. No sounds, prefixes, star identities, or plant identities were assigned.
