# Which realistic changes destroy usable support?

> Plain-English reading guide to the archived snapshot. The linked reports contain the full rules, results, and qualifications. This guide is editorial material added after the original archive.

## What we tested

Varied line length, origin position, target-word replacement, and unknown slots separately and together, using support-only constructed data.

## What we found

Even modest changes could push layouts below the unchanged support requirement. Combining length shifts and sparse identities reduced support much more than the idealized baseline suggested.

## What remains unresolved

This generated no qok outcomes and tested no manuscript association. It explains a pipeline bottleneck rather than demonstrating a writing mechanism.
