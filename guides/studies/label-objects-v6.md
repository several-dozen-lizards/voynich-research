# Can written labels be linked reliably to depicted objects?

> Plain-English reading guide to the archived snapshot. The linked reports contain the full rules, results, and qualifications. This guide is editorial material added after the original archive.

## What we tested

Developed a frozen label-to-object protocol, scientific controls, and independent image-registration pilots.

## What we found

The scientific qualification and registration gates did not pass. Neither two-pass registration pilot established usable accepted label geometry, and tiny vision-interface checks did not establish manuscript reliability.

## What remains unresolved

No manuscript label/object claim test ran. Reliable independent registration, object ratings, and successful controls were still required; failed attempts and raw responses were preserved.
