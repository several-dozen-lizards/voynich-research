# Repairing a test for differences between contexts

> Plain-English reading guide to the archived snapshot. The linked reports contain the full rules, results, and qualifications. This guide is editorial material added after the original archive.

## What we tested

Repaired a statistical baseline and checked it on constructed examples before applying the qualified version to the retrospective manuscript comparison.

## What we found

The repaired version passed its declared calibration checks. The manuscript comparison detected no within-scribe differences in effect strength.

## What remains unresolved

The test had limited ability to detect moderate differences. No detection does not establish that the effect is constant, and the text was already exposed or uncertified as untouched.
