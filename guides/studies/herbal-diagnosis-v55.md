# Why were herbal relationships being missed?

> Plain-English reading guide to the archived snapshot. The linked reports contain the full rules, results, and qualifications. This guide is editorial material added after the original archive.

## What we tested

Varied wording, reference handling, and passage boundaries in a frozen diagnosis of the herbal relation extractor.

## What we found

The variations changed the number of candidate links, but lacked independent correctness labels. Previously unprocessed source blocks supplied no recognized planets, leaving that fresh-block check inconclusive.

## What remains unresolved

More extracted links are not automatically more accurate links. Medieval transfer and independently judged recipe relationships remained unqualified.
