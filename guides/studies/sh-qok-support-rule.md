# Defining a minimum amount of usable comparison

> Plain-English reading guide to the archived snapshot. The linked reports contain the full rules, results, and qualifications. This guide is editorial material added after the original archive.

## What we tested

Proposed a support rule based on legal prefix reassignment, page coverage, and concentration, without selecting on qok outcomes.

## What we found

The admitted constructed cases met the limited declared qualification, while many sparse layouts were excluded. The rule measures usable comparisons rather than the total number of readable words.

## What remains unresolved

It was not proved to be the smallest sufficient threshold or sensitive to arbitrarily small effects. Manuscript support still had to be checked separately.
