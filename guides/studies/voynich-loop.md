# Keeping research decisions, reserves, and statistical spending traceable

> Plain-English reading guide to the archived snapshot. The linked reports contain the full rules, results, and qualifications. This guide is editorial material added after the original archive.

## What we tested

Built a staged ledger and role/custody system intended to separate exploratory proposals, frozen tests, qualification, protected scoring, and review.

## What we found

The retained checkpoint includes hash-chained records, projection checks, runner tests, and staged isolation work. Its README records that autonomous research and live model inference were not activated at that checkpoint.

## What remains unresolved

Passing software tests is not full operating-system custody or methodological qualification. Publishing this folder does not activate the loop, authorize reserve access, or make a claim test valid.
