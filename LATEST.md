## Reading the evidence

Keep engineering correctness, source fidelity, control qualification, held-out support, and manuscript conclusions separate. A result can be reproducible while its adapter semantics fail. A successful control can qualify a narrow instrument while leaving manuscript scoring closed. Negative or unsupported outcomes constrain the tested design and sample; they do not reject an entire language or mechanism class.

The archive preserves the current files and their accounting. It does not declare a decipherment or convert exploratory, retrospective, exposed-source or synthetic evidence into untouched confirmation.
