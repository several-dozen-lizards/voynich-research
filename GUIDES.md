# Plain-English research guides

Start with the [README overview](README.md) for the broad findings. Each guide below separates the question, result, unresolved issues, folder contents, and owning evidence. These guides describe the archived snapshot, including older and superseded checkpoints; they are not new experiments.

Repeated trial/seed directories are explained by their parent study. A thousand trial files do not necessarily represent a thousand independent experiments.

Results summaries describe the archived outcome. A control-only pass, a failed qualification, an unsupported comparison, and an unrun stage are different results; follow the linked guide for the limits and owning evidence.
