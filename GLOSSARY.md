# Terms used in the research archive

- **EVA:** a way of writing manuscript signs with ordinary keyboard characters. An EVA letter or string is a transcription label, not an established sound or meaning.
- **H, F, ZL, LSI:** identifiers for readings or transcription sources. They must be interpreted in their owning source records. Different readings of one manuscript do not create independent manuscripts, and a shared label does not prove independent ancestry at every location.
- **Folio; recto/verso:** a numbered manuscript leaf; its front and back. Some studies group both sides or all panels of a foldout together to avoid treating related material as independent.
- **Hand or scribe:** an attribution of handwriting to a writer. Disputed or missing attributions remain uncertain.
- **Currier A/B:** traditional labels for differing text distributions. They are not established identifications of two decoded languages.
- **Control:** a comparison with a known construction or expected behavior. A planted positive asks whether a method can detect a signal; a negative or nuisance control asks whether it gets fooled by something else.
- **Null comparison:** the specified “no extra relationship of this kind” reference used by a test. What a shuffle preserves determines what the test can distinguish.
- **Search correction:** accounting for the opportunities to find something striking when many candidates, settings, or comparisons were examined.
- **Qualification:** evidence that a particular method meets its declared checks for a specified task and scope. It is not blanket approval for every source, sample size, or interpretation.
- **Power or sensitivity:** the chance that a test detects a specified effect when that effect is present. A test with poor power can miss a real relationship.
- **Held out:** excluded from a particular fitting or selection step. Material can be held out from a model while already exposed to earlier research.
- **Untouched or protected reserve:** material with documented custody supporting the stronger claim that the relevant evidence was not previously examined. A new filename or split is not enough.
- **Retrospective:** a check made with awareness of earlier observations or using already exposed material. It can be informative without being untouched confirmation.
- **Exploratory:** a search or refinement used to generate or understand an idea. Its own outcomes do not become independent confirmation merely because the calculation is precise.
- **Not estimable / unsupported:** the required comparison lacks usable variation, overlap, or enough independent support. This is not evidence that the effect equals zero.
- **Not run / pending:** no scientific result was produced for that stage. It is different from a test that ran and failed.
- **Calibration failed:** the method did not meet a required known-answer or nuisance-control condition. The failure constrains the method before a manuscript interpretation can be trusted.
- **Bits, log loss, or predictive gain:** measures of how well a specified model predicts written data. Better prediction is not automatically better understanding or translation.
- **Baseline:** the reference method or rate used for comparison. Beating a weak reference is less informative than beating an adequate one.
- **Specificity:** a method's ability to avoid giving its intended signal to the wrong kind of constructed example. It is distinct from its ability to detect a genuine positive.
- **Homophonic encoding:** a proposed arrangement in which several written signs can represent the same underlying unit. Testing one such construction does not show that the manuscript uses it.
- **Spelling class, token, unit, or group:** an operational way of dividing or grouping the transcription. It is not automatically a syllable, word, morpheme, or historical sign category.
- **Word body:** the remaining written string after the particular opening under study has been removed. Exact-body matching compares otherwise identical remainders; it does not establish that the remainder is a linguistic root.
- **Frame / variable slot:** fixed surrounding words or spelling classes with a changing item or sequence between them. A repeated frame can arise for several reasons; recognizing one does not automatically identify a sentence template or meaning.
- **Hash / checksum / freeze:** a way of identifying exact file bytes and recording which rules or inputs were pinned. A correct hash does not prove a source claim, establish blinding, or validate an experiment's interpretation.

See the [plain-English guides](GUIDES.md) for these distinctions in context. The [latest recorded work](LATEST.md) and each study's dated reports take precedence over generic status words.
