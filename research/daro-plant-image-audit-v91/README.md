# Do daro-associated pages depict fennel?

> Plain-English reading guide to the archived snapshot. The linked reports contain the full rules, results, and qualifications. This guide is editorial material added after the original archive.

## What we tested

Inspected the plant drawings on the selected daro-opening pages before consulting the detailed reference and competing identification notes.

## What we found

No convincing fennel candidate emerged from those pages. The most suggestive feathery silhouette resolved into broad toothed leaves on closer inspection.

## What remains unresolved

The selection was exposed and not a blinded botanical identification. The result is limited to these drawings and does not reject a wider historical herb tradition.
