Reference and limits

Fennel’s reference morphology combines repeated fine leaf division into threadlike terminal segments, sheathing bases and compound umbels of small yellow flowers. No selected drawing clearly shows that combination. Architecture and leaf division carry the comparison; blue versus yellow alone does not decide it. Modern botanical morphology cannot supply a strict exclusion rule for stylized medieval drawings. Dill also shares fine foliage and umbels, so even a positive match would need further discrimination.
