What the effect sizes mean

D is the net number of sh X qok minus qok X sh. An alternative weights a compatible arrangement by r raised to its D score, with r fixed in advance. This favours higher residual ordering without changing any permitted word multiset, endpoint or adjacent class-transition count. A weight1.5 is not a prediction of50%more observed pairs.

Weights were1.10,1.25,1.5,2,3,4, with null1 and reciprocal reverse-direction controls. In the earlier two-path +/-1toy, the corresponding positive-path probability is r²/(1+r²). Each actual layout has a different mixture of score ranges; hundreds of variable spans are not interchangeable with identical toy choices.

Alternatives are view-specific mathematical models, not one shared synthetic manuscript evaluated through both views. H/F are not multiplied into an independent-replication power estimate.
