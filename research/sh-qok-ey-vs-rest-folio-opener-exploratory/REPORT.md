## Limits and verification

The matched result targets the strata with overlap, not all events in a context. Small or selective overlap can strongly limit interpretation; counts, weights and each individual stratum are supplied. Matched rates use common harmonic-exposure weights and are not simple pooled rates. Removing structurally unavailable followers changes both denominators and stratum weights.

C1/C4 are discovery/posthoc contexts; C3 motivated this refinement. All family-level outcomes were already examined, and H/F are two readings of the same manuscript. No new significance score, support gate, hypothesis denominator, model, qualification or prospective claim is introduced. Other spelling, layout and context differences remain uncontrolled; no meanings or grammar/junction conclusion follows.

Independent exact-rational regrouping from literal origins/followers reproduced all28 combined and56 opener-specific matched effects (84 checks) and every stratum count. Component opener effects recombine exactly. All28 earlier raw contrasts and group totals were reproduced. Eey exclusion and v3.1 availability were rechecked, source hashes stayed unchanged, and previous analyses remain intact.
