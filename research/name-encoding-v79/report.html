What this can establish

These controls measure consistency and unseen-group coverage in a favourable paired-name setting. A pass does not validate inferred segmentation, uncertain plant identification, alternative-name selection or phonology. No model is cleared for Voynich application by this screen alone.

Candidate corrections and f9v

Uiola returns as a conditional genus-level violet candidate, with its sweet-violet ambiguity retained.
