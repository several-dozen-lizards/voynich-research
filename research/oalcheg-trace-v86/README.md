# Tracing an unusual label and its near matches

> Plain-English reading guide to the archived snapshot. The linked reports contain the full rules, results, and qualifications. This guide is editorial material added after the original archive.

## What we tested

Searched all available readings and sections for exact and near spellings, then inspected the relevant manuscript images.

## What we found

The exact label had one physical location. Its closest botanical neighbor had disputed reading and segmentation, and a wider astronomical lead labelled a curved connector rather than an identified individual star.

## What remains unresolved

Multiple transcriptions do not multiply the number of physical attestations. No admissible historical name or sound mapping was established.
