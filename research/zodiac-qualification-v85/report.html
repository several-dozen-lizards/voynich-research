Frozen scoring

Four eligible synthetic sign shapes: Aries, Taurus, Gemini, Cancer (120 slots). Pisces has 29 visible figures; Leo has an unresolved fold figure, so neither is forced into a thirty-slot model. Six binary traversal choices × 30 shared offsets × 16 table/category hypotheses = 30,720 candidates; 18,424 distinct patterns after exact deduplication. No per-sign offset fitting.

The score is absolute correlation after centering within each panel/ring. Every one of 199 cyclic-shift nulls repeats the full search. Missing values move with their ring sequence and remain missing; observed-slot means and norms are recalculated. A further factor of two reserves correction for garment and enclosure tests. Pigment and text labels are not scored.
