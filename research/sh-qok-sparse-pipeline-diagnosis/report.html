This is a descriptive allocation for the chosen synthetic perturbation strengths, not a causal percentage for Voynich. Isolated losses need not sum to the combined loss. Restoring a factor in a heavily damaged background can produce less gain than removing it from a clean baseline costs.

Handling checks and limits

Every raw synthetic line round-trips through the frozen atlas cleaning policy. Independent eligibility agrees, baseline support is checked, shift-only lengths stay fixed, and unknown token identities retain slots. Different boundary histories remain literal. L/S use controlled padding rather than the previous mixed front/back-padding recipe so their effects can be separated.

Underlying layouts have four repeatedly occurring target bodies and designed availability patterns. Perturbations are authored stress scenarios, not calibrated historical distributions. No association test or power result follows from support loss alone. Real H/F alignment and manuscript scoring remain untouched.
