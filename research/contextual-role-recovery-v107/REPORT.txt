DIAGNOSIS AND LIMITS
Caesar cum is the strongest supported clustering result: 68.56% balanced recall versus50% null, ARI .1848. Its adjusted label-permutation p=.04 does not override its failed recovery/ARI gates. The predeclared supervised diagnostic reaches76.82% there and70.59% for Cicero letters cum. Those are label-trained diagnostic results, not blind role discovery or a new qualification. None reaches80%; this simple four-neighbor surface representation plus these algorithms has not demonstrated robust broad transfer. Low performance can reflect representation, annotation categories, lexical sparsity and clustering assumptions; it does not show that contextual distinctions are absent.

The contextual role idea remains open, but this continuation gives a concrete negative result for the attempted selector and fails to extend the narrow Casebooks result. A future method revision must be a separate prospective experiment, preserving these failures; it should not be justified by selecting a favorable panel here. No manuscript scoring automatically follows.
