More realistic structure, limited realism

The sparse generator uses variable line lengths6–14,12bodies,35% origin prevalence and2% unknown slots. The dense diagnostic uses length12,fourbodies,70% origin prevalence and no missing slots. Both create actual token sequences: sh/ch origins, other words and possible qok followers. They are authored stress cases, not fitted historical distributions.

Unlike earlier independent event arrays, a following origin cannot also be qok. Planted+.15/+.45 effects operate when a following nonorigin token is available; the marginal event-level effect is therefore diluted. In particular,70% origin density makes the dense diagnostic rich in origin comparisons but poor in available qok followers. Its low power must not be compared directly to the earlier clean +15-point event controls. This limitation is retained, not silently corrected after outcomes.

Unknown slots retain position and block adjacency. First and final origins are excluded, line boundaries never crossed, and no eligible-word repacking occurs. Exact matching was checked against20 independent exhaustive small-graph oracles; subset distributions against enumeration; token boundary/unknown fixtures passed. These checks do not constitute full corpus-parser validation.

The bottleneck is broader than the eight fixed positions. Exact-body recurrence within the same page, source-line length and paired positions remains rare. This does not prove that the complete set of margin-preserving arrangements is empty; the method still selects disjoint position pairs. Changing matching assumptions or using a more complete conditional null would be a new candidate requiring fresh qualification.

All historical failures remain intact. No threshold was relaxed, no failed trial removed, and no manuscript rescore performed.
