What was counted

Each maximal nonempty e-run is replaced by E, leaving all other symbols and run positions intact: cheol/cheeol share chEol; cheeedeeol becomes chEdEol with length vector(3,2). chol stays separate. No ch/sh collapse, sound values, zero-e merging or unknown repairs. e denotes literal EVA transcription, not an adjudicated manuscript glyph.

Variation is the unbiased probability that two distinct observed tokens of the same pattern have different run-length vectors: D=1−Σnᵥ(nᵥ−1)/(N(N−1)). Unlike the number of distinct variants, its expectation does not rise merely because one format has more tokens. Precision still depends on sample size, and repeated/copied or correlated tokens remain possible.

Main view includes all nonempty-run vectors; the predeclared sensitivity restricts to exactly one e-run. Both H/F Recipes views have zero supported same-page cells, so no inferential p-value was produced. Formal tests were limited in advance to Recipes H/F×two views, with leaf clustering and Bonferroni4 had support existed. No p-value was salvaged by moving to another panel or merging pages.

Qualification and practical outcome

Before manuscript scores, known one-/multiple-/zero-run examples were checked; diversity agreed with direct unordered-pair enumeration for124binary token samples. Exact enumeration of equal-distribution samples of sizes3and2 gives expected format gap0. A12-leaf planted difference passes the predeclared sign-flip correction; reverse and all-zero controls do not. Exact orbit tail ranks obey their bound. These qualify arithmetic and a strong synthetic signal, not real-data power or numeral interpretation.

Every saved cell diversity and coverage event count was independently recomputed. Unknown readings and all focal alternatives remain available. No image adjudication or historical names/sounds were fitted. The result is limited support for the proposed experiment, not failure of all e-run hypotheses.

The next useful evidence would be repeated, image-checked label forms with adequate transcription coverage in the same hand and page context. This audit does not promote a semantic counting model or substitute another attractive word family.
