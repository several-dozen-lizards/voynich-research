# Is the shedy–middle–shedy frame unusually recurrent?

> Plain-English reading guide to the archived snapshot. The linked reports contain the full rules, results, and qualifications. This guide is editorial material added after the original archive.

## What we tested

Tested both the frame's total count and the recurrence of distinct middle words under two position/frequency-aware null comparisons.

## What we found

One H-only middle-recurrence comparison passed one corrected screen. Total frequency did not pass, and the result did not become a consistent paired finding across readings and controls.

## What remains unresolved

The conclusion depends on the chosen comparison model. No middle word was assigned an operation, attribute, timing, or medicinal meaning.
