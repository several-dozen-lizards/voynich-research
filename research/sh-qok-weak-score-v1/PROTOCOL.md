## Target and scope

One primary question per H/F x bathing/starred panel: does distant written context change the association between sh/ch choice and immediately following qok, beyond the specified local and additive-distant nuisance models? This narrows the previous broad G-L prediction question to qok-by-context interactions. It is not a test for grammar or semantics. Long reusable templates, hidden registers, and context-dependent spelling can produce the same dependency. The earlier equal-output grammar/template counterexample remains in force.

## Honest fitting and score

Sort folios by SHA256('weak-score-honest|'+folio). The first max(4,ceil(F/4)) folios learn every nuisance model, vocabulary, projection, and direction. All remaining folios test one frozen direction. No fitting or choice uses testing folios or their outcomes. Unlike previous overlapping cross-validation, the testing folios never serve as training examples. Support requires >=10 total folios,>=6 test folios,>=100 training events,>=200 testing events,>=30 of each prefix in testing. Insufficient support is not estimable, not negative.

Nuisance features progress P,J,L as previously frozen; F adds distant word identities at offsets -4,-3,+3,+4 without qok interactions. Each stage can fall back to its predecessor, starting from add-half constant. All fits use C=.25 liblinear logistic regression, tolerance1e-7,max_iter2000. Disjoint halves of learning folios, ordered by SHA256('weak-inner|'+folio), select convex weights with the unchanged v2 derivative optimizer and one-parameter AIC-scale penalty. Refit on all learning events; test outcomes never choose weights. Comparator adequacy remains loss<=training-rate constant+.01bits/event on testing data.

For each far offset select at most12 literal words with >=8 learning occurrences, ordered by decreasing count then lexically; EDGE/UNKNOWN excluded. Construct interaction columns 1[word at offset]*(2*next_qok-1). No label-driven vocabulary selection. Project those columns onto full local plus additive-far feature matrix W, with intercept, using kernel ridge lambda=sqrt(n_learning). Residualize training and testing interaction columns with that training-only projection. Fit a ridge direction beta from residualized columns to learning (sh-indicator minus nuisance probability), again lambda=sqrt(n_learning). This bounded dimension and score test replace the broad entropy-gain hurdle. Full formulas and matrices follow core.py; they are synthetic estimators, not known grammatical representations.

On untouched testing folios form u=(y-p_null)*direction. Average u within each folio, then use a one-sided Student t statistic over independent testing-folio means with df=F_test-1. This is approximate inference relying on independent folios and adequate score calibration, not an exact randomization test. Complete synthetic pipeline qualification below is mandatory. Larger collections of dependent manuscript folios or arbitrary nuisance misspecification are not covered by those controls.

Bonferroni4 accounts for one prespecified test in each of four panels. Unlike v2's24 tests there is no position/junction/phrase gain search or separate novel-frame significance test. Also report factor24 sensitivity, and compare old/new methods at the same factor24 on paired fresh weak controls; improvement must not be attributed solely to reducing the family. No outcome-dependent test, vocabulary, split, hyperparameter, direction reversal, or additional gap distance.

## Fresh qualification, closed manuscript

Three frozen designs: (16folios,48events/folio) for exact comparability to earlier controls; (10,100) and (12,96) for approximately the previously recorded bathing/starred folio counts and event volume. These are authored uniform layouts, not a fitted model of actual coverage, clumping, lexical fragmentation, missingness, or dependence. Passing them does not establish universal manuscript-sized power.

Each design has100 fresh independent seeds per regime: position, junction, phrase, proxy_phrase, position_proxy, additive_far, weak, strong, tiny. Seeds1310000+design_index*100000+regime_index*1000+trial. The first three and weak/strong use the original source equations, with weak interaction logit coefficient.65 and strong2.5. Tiny uses.35 and is descriptive only. Qok-specific interaction is (2*far-1)*(2*qok-1). All events are nine-word lines, target index4 or3, four bodies and eight-valued local tokens, with the same literal token conventions as prior controls. Generate actual labels only from the frozen equations in run.py.

Required negative regimes: position, junction, phrase, proxy_phrase, position_proxy, additive_far. These pressure the distinction between interaction and generic predictability. They do not exhaust all positional, orthographic, or phrase mechanisms.

For EVERY design/regime required null cell, Wilson95 upper false-flag rate at factor4 must be<=.10. For EVERY weak and strong positive cell, Wilson95 lower detection rate at factor4 must be>=.80. Not estimable and inadequate-baseline trials count as nondetections for power. Report all failures and denominator counts. Tiny power, factor24 power, support, and adequacy always reported.

Engineering gates: old parser/ending/barrier fixtures, target-prefix feature exclusion, no label-driven vocabulary/splitting, independent scalar kernel-ridge residual and score calculation on a toy matrix, closed-form t replay, known zero-score result, and unchanged hashes.
