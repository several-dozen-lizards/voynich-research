# Can the proposed search recover even a known letter substitution?

> Plain-English reading guide to the archived snapshot. The linked reports contain the full rules, results, and qualifications. This guide is editorial material added after the original archive.

## What we tested

Froze unusual-word candidates but first tested the restricted search on short known-text substitution problems in Latin, Middle English, and German.

## What we found

Each language failed at least one required control, so all three language gates failed and no manuscript key was fitted.

## What remains unresolved

This is a limitation of that search and sample size, not a rejection of phonetic or rebus writing. A list of unusual forms supplies no sound values by itself.
