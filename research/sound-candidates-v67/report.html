Selected words were shso, aree, dodl, alys, eeecfhy, eteeda, shese, ypchseds, komdy, ychek, loiin, dolr, cthhy, cheoet, oloy, ykam, qakar, otas, ytchos and yfoldy. Selection used H training only; F alternatives remain full paired lines, not silently aligned or substituted.

This is not a test of all consistent sound systems. The solver searches one-to-one alphabet mappings, not syllables, homophones, pictorial name borrowing or mixtures of notation and phonetics. Isolated word samples lack the paragraph context needed for a makes-sense assessment. Random known-word control sets likewise test decipherment feasibility, not semantic coherence. Further search would need a revised recovery method qualified on fresh controls, or independently grounded candidate names; choosing plausible meanings after seeing a fit would not supply that grounding.
