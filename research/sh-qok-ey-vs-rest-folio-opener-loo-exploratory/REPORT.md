## Limits and verification

Only folio x opener strata containing both ending groups contribute. Coverage/unmatched counts and exact weights are retained for every omission in JSON/CSV; zero-weight omissions leave the estimand unchanged and are not additional evidence. Opener-specific effects are preserved separately and their weighted components exactly recombine to each combined effect.

These are post-outcome exploratory descriptions of C2/C3 selected after their negative matched differences were seen. Overlapping omissions are not independent evidence, and the observed range is not a confidence interval or statistical robustness claim. No p-values, intervals, power calculations, inferential decisions, refitting or changed gates were used. Matching controls recorded folio and opener, not every spelling or layout difference.

All8 full baselines and104 omission effects were recomputed from the event ledger and independently verified from the previously saved integer stratum counts. Including opener-specific components, 336 exact-rational comparisons agreed. Prior raw and matched analyses remain unchanged.
