# Do proposed category assignments travel to other passages?

> Plain-English reading guide to the archived snapshot. The linked reports contain the full rules, results, and qualifications. This guide is editorial material added after the original archive.

## What we tested

Kept the worksheet's proposed word categories fixed and examined their neighbors and positions on additional folios.

## What we found

The extension preserved alternate readings, unassigned words, and counterexamples instead of inventing categories for every new neighbor. The counts did not qualify a semantic model.

## What remains unresolved

The additional text came from an already inventoried corpus. Consistent-looking category substitutions still need independent evidence about what the words refer to.
