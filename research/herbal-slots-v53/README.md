# Finding possible ingredients, operations, and applications

> Plain-English reading guide to the archived snapshot. The linked reports contain the full rules, results, and qualifications. This guide is editorial material added after the original archive.

## What we tested

Used known herbal text to look for explicit cues to several kinds of recipe information, keeping later and medieval material separate.

## What we found

The extractor found candidate coverage, but recipe boundaries and semantic correctness were not independently established. A heading-contamination bug and authored semantic failures were preserved.

## What remains unresolved

The medieval sample was too small for transfer claims. The method did not qualify for manuscript scoring, and counts should not be read as accuracy.
