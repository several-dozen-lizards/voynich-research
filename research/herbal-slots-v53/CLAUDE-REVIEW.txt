Useful hypotheses, not confirmed readings:
- Keeping the whole remainder after a proposed prefix avoids deleting the candidate information along with a presumed suffix. Compare alternative segmentations; do not define the prefix as a semantic category in advance.
- Claude explicitly retracts the separate-subject-lists interpretation after within-subject controls. Preserve that retraction. Its old p<.001 is not evidence for subject lists.
- The named scripts are paths in Claude's environment, not attached runnable local scripts. Counts, entropy estimates, exact label selection and significance are not independently reproduced here.

Corrections to claims about our work:
- v50 supports qo enrichment for bath versus botanical paragraph interiors in hand2/CurrierB. It does not establish recipe meaning or confirm all three hands.
- Around150 word types and survival after top5 removal rule out only concentration in those five types. They do not rule out broader vocabulary/family composition. Remaining fold differences were about3.9–10.7pp across H/F, not uniformly10pp.
- Internal od enrichment was a matched botanical-v-bath hand2 contrast; it is not a universal herbal marker. Wheel comparisons reverse direction between some pages.
- Label enrichment and broad combinability of ot/ok would be compatible with a naming convention, but do not establish name semantics. Spelling constraints, layout and tokenizer choices compete.
- The proposed qody/arody relationship depends on explicit alternative segmentation; do not assume shared semantic core.
- EVA glyph names are transcription symbols, not phonetic or semantic identities. e-runs are not quantities merely because lengths1–3 are common. Formula degrees1–4 does not follow from a run histogram.
- More e-run variation in recipes than labels would need same-hand/format/length/family frequency controls, plus known quantity and spelling controls. Labels may contain quantities and recipes may repeat fixed terms. This is an exploratory prediction, not a discriminator by itself.

Proposed symbolic recipe language remains possible in broad form. The known-text pilot does not validate its interpretation in Voynich and does not map ot/ok/qo or endings to any of its slots.
