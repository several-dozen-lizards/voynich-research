Rare-pair component backoff v110 — 25 October 2026

RESULT
This fixed singleton-pair backoff rule produces negligible recovery change and no coverage gain against the repaired v109 baseline. Across the four prespecified adequately supported panels, equal-panel mean balanced correct-credit72.522% ->72.586% (+0.064 percentage points). Strict balanced recall66.344% ->66.408% (+0.064points). Pooled decision coverage stays577/648=89.04%; equal-panel mean coverage stays87.38%. These are distinct aggregation conventions. No full supported comparison reaches80% all-case recovery.

One additional correct decision accounts for the entire primary recovery gain. Across all eight panels, including the four under-supported ones, only3/808 decisions change: one wrong->correct, two correct->wrong. All126 zero-evidence cases remain abstentions. None of the paired recovery deltas exceeds its shuffled-control benchmark after the declared descriptive Bonferroni8 correction (all adjustedp=1). Keep v109 as the current development baseline; do not promote this backoff variant on these results.

WHY LITERAL FALLBACK WOULD NOT CHANGE ANYTHING
The existing representation already contains four component word-at-position features (-2,-1,+1,+2 around the target) and two same-side context-pair features (-2/-1 and+1/+2). The pairs are not target-neighbor bigrams. The repaired scorer already assigns zero contribution to globally unknown pairs. Adding the same component words again on fallback would duplicate their evidence and change their weights. Without duplication, fallback from an unseen pair is exactly the existing baseline. This identity was verified for every actual scored occurrence.

THE ONE FROZEN NONTRIVIAL RULE
Rare means pooled binary-role TRAINING count exactly1. Repeated support begins at2. Components always remain present once. Singleton pair features do not contribute extra evidence; pairs with count>=2 remain active. No threshold grid or after-result tuning was performed. Boundary tokens in original features remain subject to the same declared rule, not silently exempted.

The unmodified v109 repaired score function is imported directly. Both arms share the exact baseline-fitted counts, vocabulary, class totals, smoothing denominators, equal priors and1e-12 tie tolerance. This is activation-only backoff: it isolates the contribution of singleton pairs without retraining a changed feature inventory that could alter unrelated word scores. It does not test all possible interpolated/backoff language models.

Data, role definitions, the whole-block split, all four original component-word features, the four supported panel identities and supervised training labels stay fixed. No window expansion, cross-position sharing, new word form, lemma, morphology, dependency label, new classifier or clustering change was introduced. All v107/v109 saved artifacts retain their hashes.

HOW MUCH DID THE RULE ACTUALLY TOUCH?
63/808 test occurrences contain a recognized singleton pair (64 pair events). The other745 had no singleton term to remove. In the supported panels,49/648 occurrences are touched (50 events). Most sparse pair cases were already unseen and neutral. Removing pairs cannot create new recognized word evidence because their components were already included. Every original zero-evidence/support status is unchanged. Although a score tie could theoretically change decision coverage, no such tie transition occurred in this run.

INTERPRETATION AND NEXT DIRECTION
This test does not support adopting singleton suppression. It also does not reject every pair-backoff or interpolation scheme. The specific obstacle remains that the component-word representation is already present and often unsupported: subtracting a pair cannot add lexical coverage. A future representation experiment should explicitly introduce a train-learned way to share component-word evidence, while preserving positional information separately and keeping the repaired scorer as the comparison baseline. That is a new prospective question; it was not fitted or scored here. An independently selected confirmation source is still needed before broad transfer claims. No sealed reserve from another branch was opened.
