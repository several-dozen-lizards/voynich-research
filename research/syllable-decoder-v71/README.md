# Trying a restricted whole-written-syllable mapping

> Plain-English reading guide to the archived snapshot. The linked reports contain the full rules, results, and qualifications. This guide is editorial material added after the original archive.

## What we tested

Required each cipher group to stand for a complete candidate written syllable and tested known-answer controls before manuscript application.

## What we found

None of the three fresh controls qualified. Missing inventory coverage and poor recovery persisted even with true group boundaries supplied.

## What remains unresolved

Only one restricted Latin method and syllable definition were tested. The failure does not reject syllabic writing, and no manuscript scores were produced.
