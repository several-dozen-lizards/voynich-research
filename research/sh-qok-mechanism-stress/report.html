What the controls establish

Page preferences vary sh and qok prevalence together between pages without creating a local rule. Junction spelling changes ch to sh when the next token begins qok. Repeated phrases insert shedy qokam and chedy dar. Neither latter generator contains grammar, but both contain real local dependence: detection is correct for association and insufficient for identifying its cause.

The interior-position adversary places sh and qok in successive fixed interior slots without a word-selection rule. It is intentionally the same fixture in all 100 runs; only permutation seeds change. Its counts measure Monte Carlo repeatability, not performance on 100 independent datasets. The automatically saved Wilson interval for that regime must not be interpreted as population uncertainty. No qualification gate was assigned to it. Endpoint preservation does not condition on interior ordinal position.

The synthetic examples use only the edy family, so ending-family and exact-body views coincide; agreement between those columns adds no independent evidence. These controls do not measure power at the manuscript's observed effect size or rule out all nuisance processes.

Research status

Keep the bath result as a retrospective, local conditional association. Do not promote it to a grammatical consequence, a thing/action reading, or an isolated causal effect of sh. Existing one-gap failures do not establish a junction mechanism. No manuscript page was rescored, no reserved sign was opened, and no fresh holdout was claimed: these panels have already been exposed.

Before stronger manuscript inference, a new method would need qualification against interior position and lexical or phrase context. That revision has not been selected or run here. Claude's original gap.py remains unavailable, so this is not an exact reproduction of that script.
