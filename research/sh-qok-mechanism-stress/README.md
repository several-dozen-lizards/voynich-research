# Could non-grammatical mechanisms produce the association?

> Plain-English reading guide to the archived snapshot. The linked reports contain the full rules, results, and qualifications. This guide is editorial material added after the original archive.

## What we tested

Reused the existing manuscript audit and tested new constructed page, position, junction, and other local mechanisms against its comparison rules.

## What we found

The bath association remained a recorded structural finding, but the stress tests showed why controlling some nuisances does not uniquely identify its cause. Stronger exact-body evidence was mixed across readings.

## What remains unresolved

Grammar, contextual spelling, junction effects, and collocations were not separated conclusively. No new manuscript fit was used to rescue the claim.
