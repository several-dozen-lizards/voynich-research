# Looking at the ey ending by itself

> Plain-English reading guide to the archived snapshot. The linked reports contain the full rules, results, and qualifications. This guide is editorial material added after the original archive.

## What we tested

Made a retrospective breakdown of next-qok rates for the original ey ending family across the recorded contexts, keeping eey separate.

## What we found

The saved counts show substantial context differences and retain the separate sh/ch arms and availability-restricted views. This was a description after earlier family results had been seen.

## What remains unresolved

No significance tests or confidence intervals were calculated. The counts do not establish an ey meaning, a universal suppression rule, or independent confirmation.
