## Origin (exploratory, E)

In attempt C's six Rosettes panels (f85r1, f85r2, f86v3–v6), origins ending **-ey** and **-eey** had an sh−ch difference of exactly **+0.00** in both H and F, with real comparison weight (ey 11–14%, eey 2–3%). That pattern is most likely what you get when **no qok follows any -ey/-eey origin**, after sh or after ch. Over the same six panels, the -edy, -y and other families showed +8 to +10 points.
