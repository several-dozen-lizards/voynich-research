# Preparing a diagnosis of the syllable decoder

> Plain-English reading guide to the archived snapshot. The linked reports contain the full rules, results, and qualifications. This guide is editorial material added after the original archive.

## What we tested

Specified objective, search, coverage, and sample-size checks for the failed syllable method, with frozen fresh-control mappings.

## What we found

The retained folder contains the protocol, source-coverage information, inventories, keys, and diagnostic setup. It does not contain a completed result report or a verified successful follow-up.

## What remains unresolved

The preceding failure remains intact. Proposed refinements and prepared keys are not evidence that syllable recovery was repaired or that manuscript scoring opened.
