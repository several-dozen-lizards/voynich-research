# Where was the longer-gap test losing sensitivity?

> Plain-English reading guide to the archived snapshot. The linked reports contain the full rules, results, and qualifications. This guide is editorial material added after the original archive.

## What we tested

Held the planted alternative fixed while varying the search correction, separating the cost of conditioning from the cost of a broad search.

## What we found

The diagnostic quantified how the full search family and error budget reduced detection. Easier counterfactual screens could detect more, but were not substituted for the frozen acceptance rule.

## What remains unresolved

The earlier qualification remained failed. No robustness bank was resumed and no manuscript was rescored.
