# Distinguishing limited certification precision from a demonstrated bias

> Plain-English reading guide to the archived snapshot. The linked reports contain the full rules, results, and qualifications. This guide is editorial material added after the original archive.

## What we tested

Diagnosed the small earlier control banks, then retained a separately frozen, much larger follow-up and a permutation-bank diagnosis of the old heterogeneity test.

## What we found

The completed follow-up qualified the declared A/Q1 cells under its constructed scope. The original near-miss statuses remained preserved, and extra inner shuffles did not automatically resolve the separate B-Q2 problem.

## What remains unresolved

The root diagnostic report precedes the completed run. More precise control certification does not make exposed manuscript material fresh or automatically promote previously blocked results.
