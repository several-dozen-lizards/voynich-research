RETROSPECTIVE DIAGNOSIS — v3.1 preserved; frozen follow-up proposed, not executed.

The P1-F and bath failures are certification-precision failures in the observed 200-trial experiments, with no demonstrated excess above the 5% tolerance. This does not establish that every generator is correctly calibrated; it identifies what the existing failures can support. The original statuses remain unchanged.

Every listed empirical rate is below the 5% tolerance. Every listed interval still includes the Part A nominal rejection threshold, 0.05/3 = 1.6667%. Thus none of these individual failures establishes either practical inflation above 5% or nominal-size inflation. For a Binomial(200, 1/60) benchmark, observing at least four flags has probability 42.76%; at least five has probability 24.25%. These are descriptive calculations, not a selected set of new hypothesis tests.

The old Wilson gate accepts at most three flags. At a true rate of 1/60, its chance of passing is 57.24% per scenario, and 6.14% across five independent scenarios at that boundary. At the Part B nominal rate of 2.5%, the corresponding values are 26.15% per scenario and 0.467% across four independent B-Q1 scenarios. These are boundary benchmarks, not estimated pass probabilities for the actual discrete tests: exact tests may be conservative, and joint probability depends on dependence. They show why an engine can be correctly calibrated and still miss this small-simulation certification design.

I independently reconstructed the conditional distribution using explicit combinatorial probabilities rather than the production SciPy hypergeometric routine. All 181 audited trials matched, including every null false flag in the saved A/Q1 controls and one nonflag from each null cell/scenario. Maximum absolute p-value error was 2.22×10⁻¹⁶; outcome hashes and threshold decisions matched. This is strong arithmetic evidence for these trials, not universal proof of the implementation. Full records are in DIAGNOSIS.json.

For A/Q1, the per-trial p-values are exact convolutions, so there is no inner permutation Monte Carlo to increase. The uncertainty comes from the 200 outer synthetic outcome draws. For constant low/high rates, conditional hypergeometric inference is appropriate under within-stratum exchangeability. The junction-only generator is also constant within each ending family: its .10/.60 rate depends only on final y, and the family definition separates final-y from other origins. After dropping blocked events, A’s availability-only control has the same marginal law as low-null, on a different seed stream. It is an independent repetition of that law, not a second availability mechanism. Additive line/position variation is a separate robustness stress because exact exchangeability is not guaranteed; it did not fail any A/Q1 cell here. See the [official R description of exact conditional inference](https://search.r-project.org/R/refmans/stats/html/fisher.test.html).

B-Q1’s ordinary low/high/junction controls also miss the old precision gate, with 4–7 flags. Its additional availability-only stress has 24/200 H and 22/200 F flags, but that is not a sharp null for its unconditioned aggregate estimand. Equal available qok rates of .20 produce an analytically expected aggregate difference of +1.284 H / +1.166 F percentage points on this geometry. Moreover, the inherited ordinary B-Q1 generators permit qok successes on structurally blocked followers: they qualify an abstract Bernoulli model, not a physically faithful token generator. These limitations must stay visible. Testing available-only Currier A would be a distinct manuscript question and is outside the proposed calibration replication.

B-Q2 cannot be diagnosed as the same near miss. Its homogeneous-null counts are 7/200 in each reading; homogeneous-positive counts are 10/200 H and 8/200 F. The exact two-sided 95% interval for 10/200 is 2.423%–9.003%, still spanning its 2.5% nominal level and the 5% tolerance. It warrants investigation, but does not establish inflation after accounting for the multiple cells inspected. All ten H homogeneous-positive flags have pointwise inner-tail intervals wholly below .025, so simply increasing inner permutation count is unlikely to explain that particular count. These inner checks are descriptive, not simultaneous confidence statements.

Four F homogeneous-positive trials have a defined observed statistic but exactly one undefined statistic among the 10,000 saved shuffles; the original method correctly abstained under its frozen rule. One F heterogeneous-positive trial does too. Whether this is genuine zero variance or numerical cancellation remains unresolved; the follow-up requires direct centered-residual and high-precision checks. Increasing the number of shuffles can make rare undefined assignments more likely to be encountered. It is not a repair for a statistic that is genuinely undefined on part of its permutation space. Whole folios also differ in geometry and sampling variance, so equal effects alone do not ensure exchangeability of their context labels.

The frozen proposal is in PROTOCOL.json, with exhaustive cell/scenario assignments in JOBS.json and source/protocol hashes in FREEZE.json. It has not generated new synthetic outcomes or scored manuscript material.

1. Preserve all v3.1 files and statuses. Use fresh outcomes on all eight A/Q1 reading-cells, including the previously passing ones. Never pool the old 200 with the fresh series or rerun only failed seeds/cells.
2. Run exactly 10,000 trials for each of 38 required-null cell/scenarios and 2,000 for each of eight positives: 396,000 fixed trials. No early success, extra batches, seed replacement, effect enlargement or stopping when a bound crosses the target. Seeds are fixed coordinate sequences in JOBS.json; geometry, available filter, exact engine, effect sizes and testing thresholds are unchanged.
3. Compute simultaneous exact Clopper–Pearson intervals, Bonferroni over the 46 null/power proportions, for family coverage at least 95%. This corrects uncertainty statements about qualification, not the manuscript Holm families. Keep the old pointwise Wilson interval as a descriptive bridge. [NIST documents exact binomial confidence limits.](https://itl.nist.gov/div898/software/dataplot/refman2/auxillar/exacbino.htm)
4. For each null, U≤.05 certifies the practical tolerance; L>.05 establishes a problem above tolerance; anything between is inconclusive. Separately, L above the test’s nominal threshold flags nominal-size excess. An exchangeable-null nominal alarm blocks nominal-calibration qualification pending investigation, even if its rate is below the 5% practical tolerance. Absence of an alarm is not proof of exact calibration.
5. Require the positive lower bound ≥.80, unchanged. A positive upper bound <.80 establishes insufficient power for that scenario; an interval straddling .80 is inconclusive. Require every cell’s necessary null and power conditions; keep H and F separate.
6. Diagnose B-Q2 separately by reconstructing all 800 existing homogeneous trials, comparing the old permutation bank with two independently seeded 10,000-shuffle banks per trial. Keep undefined trials in the denominator, identify the failing contexts, and distinguish zero variance from cancellation. This phase cannot qualify B-Q2, smooth its statistic, change its permutation scheme or rescue a manuscript result. Any method repair needs a separately frozen candidate and fresh controls.

The sample-size choice was checked analytically before any fresh outcomes. With these simultaneous intervals and N=10,000, at most 429 flags certify U≤.05. Certification probability is effectively 100% at a true rate of 1.667% or 2.5%, about 93.27% at 4%, and only 0.0476% at 5%. Thus the design has room to distinguish nominal calibration from a practically inflated rate rather than repeatedly failing for lack of precision. It still allows an inconclusive result near the tolerance boundary. PLANNING.json records the calculations.

A successful synthetic follow-up would establish a new, separately labeled qualification result. It would not rewrite v3.1, make exposed manuscript material prospective, or automatically promote any blocked manuscript cell. Manuscript re-adjudication requires a separate frozen linkage to the new qualification and remains retrospective.
