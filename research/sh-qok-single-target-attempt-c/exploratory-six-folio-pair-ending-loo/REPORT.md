## Interpretation and verification

All75 joint omissions retain a positive descriptive sign in each reading. No tested two-folio/one-family union is necessary for that sign. This describes influence rather than statistical robustness or generality.

F f85r2 and f86v3 carry zero matched comparison weight; their nominal omissions can duplicate results from smaller omission sets. Many other grid cells share most observations. All150 combinations are overlapping descriptions, not independent evidence, and their range is not a sampling-uncertainty interval.
