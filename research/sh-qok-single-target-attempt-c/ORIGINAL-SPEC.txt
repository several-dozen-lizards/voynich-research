# Voynich spec: sh→qok single-target test on unsearched panels

**For:** a fresh session, running alongside the main qualification thread.
**Written:** 24 Oct 2026 (Reed, for Re).
**Status:** frozen design. Run it as written. Anything you change becomes a new attempt: save the original, record why, and qualify the change before you look at outcomes.

---

## 0. Why this test exists (context you won't have)

- **The lead.** In Voynich bathing (Davis hand 2) and starred (hand 3) paragraph text, a word opening ***sh-*** is followed directly by a word opening ***qok-*** more often than chance. This passed a max-T search correction against a within-line shuffle (v77). In bathing, within the same page and the same ending family, *sh-* is followed by *qok-* more often than *ch-* is (v94: +10.8 points in reading H, +8.7 in F).
- **The obstacle.** Every later test has run in the same panels where the lead was found, as part of wide searches. A frozen power diagnostic (v99) showed that this search correction costs about **80 points of power**. Asking one pre-specified question detects a weight-1.5 alternative 86–95% of the time. The full ×28 search detects it 3–16% of the time.
- **What we know about the mechanism.** Two designs found no far-context dependence beyond local predictors (context-transfer attempt B; the nuisance-predictor repair, 0/4 panels). So the relationship looks **local** (adjacent words). Whether it is junction spelling or grammar is unresolved. In attempt B, the junction-feature gain (J−P) was positive in all four panels, but no panel passed correction. That pattern is post hoc.
- **The point of this test.** Ask **one pre-specified question** on material where the lead was **not discovered**. This is the first test that is neither circular nor eaten by the search correction.

## 1. House rules (unchanged project rules)

1. Freeze before looking. Write the protocol, panel list, support counts and hashes to disk **before** you compute any qok outcome.
2. Keep every failure: failed gates, failed attempts, not-estimable results.
3. Label anything designed after seeing data as **exploratory (E)**.
4. **H and F are separate readings of the same manuscript.** They are not independent samples. Score each separately. Never pool them.
5. "Not estimable" is not a negative result. A stored p = 1 from a degenerate computation is not evidence.
6. No sound values, meanings, plant IDs or glyph merges.

## 2. Data

- **Transcription:** `text16e6.original.evt` (Landini–Stolfi interlinear, EVA). Use reading codes **H** and **F**.
- **Scribes and sections:** `Davis-hands.csv` (columns `Folio, Section, Scribe, Language`).
- **Tokenisation:** use the project's existing qualified extractor (the one used in v94/v98). Word separators are `. - = ,`. Drop `{…}` comments and `!`. Any word containing `*` or `%` is unknown. Unknown words keep their slot and block adjacency.

## 3. The question (primary, one per reading)

> **H1:** within page and within ending family, P(next word opens `qok`) after an **sh-** origin **>** P(next word opens `qok`) after a **ch-** origin.
> One-sided. Direction fixed: sh > ch.

**Definitions:**

- **Origin:** a paragraph word (P-unit rows only) that opens with `sh` or `ch`. Exclude `cth`, `ckh`, `cph` and `cfh` openings. The origin must not be line-final.
- **Follower:** the next word on the **same line**. Never cross a line break. If the follower is unknown, exclude the event and count it under `following_unknown`.
- **Outcome:** follower opens `qok`.
- **Ending family:** take the origin's ending, matched longest first, from `eey`, `edy`, `ey`, `y`, otherwise `other`. Check the matching order explicitly; there was an eey/ey bug once before. Keep `other` as its own family. Do not drop it.
- **Stratum:** folio × ending family. Shuffle sh/ch labels **within stratum** only.

## 5. Support gate (outcome-blind)

With qok outcomes **masked**, compute for each panel × reading:

- eligible origins (sh and ch counts);
- the number of supported strata (strata containing both sh and ch origins);
- **V**, the conditional variance or support statistic used by the qualified exact engine;
- the largest single-page share of support;
- exclusion counts (`unknown_origin`, `origin_at_end`, `following_unknown`).

**Gate, matching the qualified band:** V ≥ 80, at least 8 supported pages, and no page supplying more than 25% of support.

- If a panel × reading fails the gate, report it as **NOT ESTIMABLE** and stop for that cell.
- Do not pool panels, merge pages, add sections, relax thresholds or swap in another panel to rescue it.
- If **both** readings of P1 fail, the primary result is NOT ESTIMABLE. That is a valid, reportable outcome.

Write `SUPPORT.json` and hash it **before** any outcome is computed.

## 6. Engine and inference

- **Primary:** the qualified exact conditional test from the V ≥ 80 support-band qualification (PASS_LIMITED_SCOPE) and the four-body boundary control, unchanged. One-sided, sh > ch.
- **Correction:** none within a cell, because there is one question. Across the pre-declared cells (P1-H, P1-F, P2-H, P2-F), use **Holm over 4**, declared now.
- **Pass rule for the primary claim:** P1-H **and** P1-F each have Holm-adjusted p < .05, with the effect in the declared direction in both. If one reading passes and the other doesn't, report "reading-dependent, not confirmed."
- **Effect reporting:** the within-stratum Mantel–Haenszel risk difference in percentage points, with a folio-bootstrap 95% CI, for every cell, including P0 (descriptive).
- **Sensitivity only (not a pass/fail input):** the repaired nuisance predictor (leave-one-learning-folio-out blend; PASS_LIMITED), applied to the same cells. Report it, but it cannot rescue a failed primary.

## 7. Secondary question (pre-declared, separate)

> **H2:** the junction model J adds predictive gain over the body/position model P (J−P > 0) for the qok outcome after sh/ch origins.

- Use the attempt-B model definitions for P and J **unchanged**, with folio-grouped cross-validation as in attempt B.
- Panels: P1 and P2, H and F separately.
- One-sided, J−P > 0. **Holm over 4**, a separate family from H1.
- Note that H2 is motivated by an exploratory pattern (all four attempt-B panels were positive and none passed). Its result is a first confirmatory test of that pattern on new panels.
- The same support gate applies. If attempt B's adequacy gate fails, report "inadequate baseline". That is not a negative.

## 8. Required controls before any outcome

Re-run, on synthetic data sized to the **actual P1/P2 support counts** from §5 (not the old uniform sizes):

- **null** (independent qok at low and high base rates);
- **additive page/line/position null**;
- **junction-only generator** (a qok rate set by the origin's final sign, with no sh/ch effect);
- **positive** (+15 points after sh).

**Gates (unchanged):** null upper Wilson-95 bound ≤ .05; positive lower bound ≥ .80. If the positive gate fails at the real support sizes, the panel is **underpowered**. Report that and still run the test, labelled as underpowered. Do not change the design.

## 10. What this test can and cannot say

- **A pass** means the sh>ch → qok dependency holds in a hand-2 (or hand-3) panel where it was not discovered. That makes it the project's first confirmatory, non-circular local-structure result.
- **A pass cannot** distinguish grammar from junction spelling. H2 and the junction-only control speak to that, partly.
- **No pass at adequate power** means the effect is probably confined to bath/starred text, or weaker elsewhere than +15 points. It **cannot** exclude smaller effects.
- **Not estimable** means the panel lacks enough comparisons. It says nothing about the effect.

**Do not:** open the zodiac reserved signs; score or rescore bath/starred inferentially; add contexts after seeing support; merge H/F; assign meanings.
