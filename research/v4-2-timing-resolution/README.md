# Recording a missed timing requirement honestly

> Plain-English reading guide to the archived snapshot. The linked reports contain the full rules, results, and qualifications. This guide is editorial material added after the original archive.

## What we tested

Audited what registration work occurred before the requested amendment freeze and checked the retained source-only geometry and hold records.

## What we found

The timing requirement had been missed. The resolution preserved the evidence and explicit later authorization with a disclosed exception, rather than backdating compliance.

## What remains unresolved

Source-only registration receipts do not establish that the entire cohort was independently re-reviewed. No scientific image/text result was created by this timing audit.
