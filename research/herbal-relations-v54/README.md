# Can a known herbal support reliable relationship extraction?

> Plain-English reading guide to the archived snapshot. The linked reports contain the full rules, results, and qualifications. This guide is editorial material added after the original archive.

## What we tested

Tested rules connecting an herb entry to ingredients, planetary associations, preparation, and application while allowing abstention on uncertain references.

## What we found

The pilot recovered candidate relationships but failed one authored control and lacked independent historical labels. Its reused source reserve was not untouched confirmation.

## What remains unresolved

Entry-level association cannot simply be inherited by every ingredient. No Voynich relationship scoring was opened.
