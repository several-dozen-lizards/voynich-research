# Controlling both line composition and exact position

> Plain-English reading guide to the archived snapshot. The linked reports contain the full rules, results, and qualifications. This guide is editorial material added after the original archive.

## What we tested

Permitted matched prefix swaps that preserve page, line composition, exact positions, and literal word bodies.

## What we found

The specified nuisance controls passed, but smaller and sparse planted effects failed broad sensitivity requirements. Conditioning on more information removed much of the usable variation.

## What remains unresolved

Correct nuisance handling does not ensure useful power. No manuscript result was produced or rescored here.
