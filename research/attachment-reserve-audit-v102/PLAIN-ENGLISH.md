# Did saved attachments contain genuinely untouched passages?

> Plain-English reading guide to the archived snapshot. The linked reports contain the full rules, results, and qualifications. This guide is editorial material added after the original archive.

## What we tested

Audited saved research attachments and their exposure history for a protected word-sequence test reserve.

## What we found

No genuine protected passage reserve was established. Some references to held-out material concerned image judgments or a model's training split, rather than text nobody had previously inspected.

## What remains unresolved

An untouched annotation can still concern already exposed text. The audit did not authorize new scoring or manufacture a fresh reserve.
