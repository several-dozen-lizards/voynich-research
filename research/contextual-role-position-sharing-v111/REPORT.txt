THE FROZEN REPRESENTATION
Baseline has six feature events: four exact word-at-position features for offsets-2,-1,+1,+2, plus exact left and right same-side pair features.
Shared retains those SIX unchanged and adds four events: W:surface_word pooled across all four positions. Repeated occurrences preserve multiplicity. For boundary markers, the added event remains position-specific, W{i}:#edge or W{i}:#barrier. No position-only constant is added. This retains explicit word-position and pair-order information while allowing literal component identity to contribute at a previously unseen position.
The duplicate control also has ten events, but its added features are W{i}:surface_word at every position. It duplicates existing position-specific information without sharing identities. It is a diagnostic control, not a winner selected after scoring. No singleton suppression from v110 was adopted.

SCORER AND TRAINING
The exact v109 repaired score function is imported unchanged: globally unseen features contribute zero, class-specific absence retains add-one smoothing, equal priors and1e-12 tie tolerance remain. Each representation learns counts/vocabulary from the same original binary-role training rows. Baseline totals are6events per training occurrence; shared and duplicate totals are10. The representation therefore changes vocabulary and smoothing normalizers as well as available evidence; model parameters were not falsely described as fixed. All24 models and their counts are saved.

No gold POS, dependency, lemma, morphology, target-head information, semantic embedding, new source word, wider window, normalization or stemming enters any representation. All original808 binary-role test IDs, gold labels, whole-block split and panel support statuses are preserved. No Voynich data or other branches' sealed reserves were accessed.

DECISION AND NEXT QUESTION
Keep v109 as the conservative reference and preserve v111 as a decision/coverage candidate. Do not adopt its raw normalized probabilities as trusted confidence, and do not call this independent qualification. The next focused question is whether shared-word fallback can retain the new coverage without counting the same recognized word through both local and shared channels. That would require a separately frozen representation test; it was not implemented or tuned here. Independent-source confirmation remains necessary before broader transfer claims.
