What each control preserves

Exact slot (primary): locks all qol and unknown slots; exchanges other words only within the same page, line length, exact ordinal and unknown-slot mask. This preserves frequency conditional on those positions. It does not preserve every source line’s word multiset.

Within line (sensitivity): preserves each line’s words, first/last words, every qol, and unknowns as barriers. Other words shuffle within readable interior spans. This controls broad position, but not every other word’s exact interior ordinal.

Selection correction: within each reading, all literal qoX/X pairs present anywhere in the fixed stratum, all single- and two-pair choices, and the full inventory are considered, in both directions. A pooled-rank minP calibration corrects that family. A further factor of four covers H/F × the two nulls. This addresses the declared family, not every anchor, spelling rule or earlier research choice.
