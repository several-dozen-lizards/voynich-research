# Phase 10 — STOP at source gate

2026-10-27. **9 admitted confident picture pairs; 15 required.** The frozen source ledger contains 35 localized proposals: 9 confident, 24 tentative, and 2 contested under the predeclared unresolved-alternative rule. Tentative and contested rows do not enter the primary set. Calibration, ZL/LSI H extraction, all permutations and S1–S3 target scoring were NOT RUN. No candidate native name loci have been established.

The count is the number verified and admitted in this cited audit, not proof that only nine confident correspondences exist in the literature. The wider Knowles/rating workbook was not publicly exposed by the retrieved forum rendering. Its aggregate totals cannot supply missing item-level evidence. A further strongly described f99r–f96v proposal has unresolved item identity/possible overlap with an existing row; even counting it as an additional confident pair would give ten, still below fifteen. It was not silently promoted or double-counted.

## Registration and freeze

[PROTOCOL.md](PROTOCOL.md) and its SHA-256 were saved in [REGISTRATION.json](REGISTRATION.json) before item-level collation and any generation/scoring. Initial bibliography searches and reads of earlier registration documents preceded that registration. [SOURCE_FREEZE.json](SOURCE_FREEZE.json) fixes the source ledger, confidence rules and provenance after collation. These are separate receipts; registration does not pretend the source list existed before it was compiled.

The protocol defines the single/multi-label rule, ZL primary and H alternate separation, paragraph-initial handling, normalized Levenshtein similarity, and a permutation-rank maximum over S1–S3. Conditional calibration retains all twelve requested condition/rate cells, fifty replicates each, and 999 permutations. At p=0, the ≤5% gate means at most 2/50 rejections; p=.25 and .5 each require at least 40/50 in every condition. No calibration dependency or implementation was substituted after this source failure.

## Exposure limitation

No local Voynich transcription, token table, concordance or target image was opened for Phase 10. However, web search snippets incidentally displayed native-word examples and prior label/page comparisons; the prior-work article also includes native-word examples and previous textual outcomes. **The literal source-stage requirement that no Voynich text be visible was therefore not fully achieved.** No exposed strings were entered into the match ledger or used for label selection/scoring. This is a source-only collation with an explicit exposure limitation, not an investigator-blind freeze. [EXPOSURE_AND_RETRIEVAL.json](EXPOSURE_AND_RETRIEVAL.json) preserves the distinction and retrieval failures.

## Relation to prior work

The picture correspondences reproduce an established line of work attributed by Zandbergen to Petersen and Stolfi and expanded by Knowles and forum participants. This audit does not introduce those visual connections.

The textual bridge is also a prior question: [Knowles' September 2023 explanation](https://www.voynich.ninja/archive/index.php/thread-4067-2.html) proposes comparing herbal text with corresponding pharmaceutical labels; [Karlov's August 2026 proposal](https://www.voynich.ninja/thread-6044.html) discusses localized label tests and panel-level dependence. These precede this registration.

[Silvio de Carvalho's September 2026 report](https://silviodc.de/ai-projects/voynich/) describes an eleven-pair comparison with tiered image confidence and a negative textual result. That is self-reported prior work, not independently reproduced here. Its use of pharmaceutical-page labels and a different control scheme is not this registered nearest-label / S1–S3 / calibration design. This Phase 10 design would extend that question if admitted; **the present source STOP neither replicates nor contradicts its textual outcome**.

No interpretation of meaning follows. The current result is insufficient verified source support to run the requested experiment under its frozen minimum, with the web-exposure limitation preserved.
