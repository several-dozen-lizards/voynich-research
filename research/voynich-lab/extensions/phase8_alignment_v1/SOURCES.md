# Phase8 sources

Naibbe: Michael A. Greshko (2025), The Naibbe cipher: a substitution cipher that encrypts Latin and Italian as Voynich Manuscript-like ciphertext, Cryptologia, DOI https://doi.org/10.1080/01611194.2025.2566408 ; author implementation https://github.com/greshko/naibbe-cipher commit719f2f4c4d1eacf95fabf48381cd84fc6cd94279. Original naibbe.py and naibbe_tables.csv reused read-only from ../phase7_two_table_v1 and copied here with original copyright/license/citation notices. These glyph tables were designed using Voynich statistics and are NOT blind controls. No key/glyph/table search occurs.

Phase8 uses TWO actual named tables, alpha and beta1, as deterministic separate keys, not different mixtures over the same six tables. Fixed single-table encryption keeps published unigram/prefix/suffix mappings and automatic normalization/respacing17/36. No stochastic deck or ambiguity redrawing is possible with a fixed key; preserve collisions under the author's UNAMBIGUOUS=False option. Direct synthetic encoding must match author encryption with a fixed single-table deck and rejection off. Exact codebooks/collisions/hashes are in FIXED_KEYS.json.

C1 Latin: Hildegard Causae et curae, Kaiser1903 public-domain edition. C2 Italian: Mattioli I discorsi, Venice1568 public-domain edition. Original source-slice/body/normalization rules, uncorrected OCR and citations remain in voynich-lab/SOURCES.md and the previous Phase7 source receipts. Reconstruct the same34509-word normalized input, apply the pinned Naibbe clean_line identically, then use disjoint69018-character development/qualification spans. All raw/normalized hashes are retained. Known source-unit streams are only encoding truth, never graph-mapping supervision.

Layout/A-B map and physical collation: frozen Phase7 metadata and physical map, originally sourced from Rene Zandbergen https://www.voynich.nu/layout.html and individual quire descriptions. Panels/sides/conjugates are grouped together in sheet folds; within-quire physical tests assume no original reading order. Physical positive fixtures intentionally clone source units within known conjugate pairs; normal same-source controls do not. Quire coherence and book-wide conjugacy are explicitly retired.

SciPy quadratic_assignment/FAQ from the installed analysis runtime, used through its installed primary implementation/docstring (source and runtime metadata recorded). FAQ is approximate, NP-hard graph matching is not globally solved; fixed starts/budget are frozen. Rank-order and native-completion baseline candidates are retained alongside four fixed FAQ starts. No alternative optimizer is selected after outcomes.

Old IID noise model/injector and all earlier Phase7/C12/C16 failures/holds are read-only sources. Only identifiers, metadata, source bytes/hashes and control text are available before the Phase8 gate. Existing target graphs/text are not consulted unless the complete new qualification passes.
