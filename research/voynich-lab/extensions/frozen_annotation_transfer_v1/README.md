# Do saved letter maps transfer to unused annotations?

> Plain-English reading guide to the archived snapshot. The linked reports contain the full rules, results, and qualifications. This guide is editorial material added after the original archive.

## What we tested

Applied two existing maps without adding assignments to annotation locations outside the month-fit folios, keeping competing readings and unknown letters explicit.

## What we found

Neither map recovered a complete multi-letter word in the admitted readings. The immediate finding was limited alphabet coverage.

## What remains unresolved

The locations were unused in that fit rather than pristine blind data. Without independently established plaintext answers, translation accuracy was not estimable and the broader month relationship was not disproved.
