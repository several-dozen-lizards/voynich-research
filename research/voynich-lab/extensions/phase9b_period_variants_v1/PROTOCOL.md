# Phase 9b — month annotation language test with period variation

Design registration: 2026-10-27. Separate from Phase 9; never overwrite its results, inputs, reports or calibration receipts. Native-script tracks remain CLOSED. Previously reported annotation readings and Phase 9 outcomes are already known: target sealed here means no Phase 9b target fit before qualification, not investigator blinding.

## Source stage and variant freeze

Primary languages: Latin, Italian, German, Occitan, Catalan. Old/Middle French is a separate secondary comparison and never enters the primary winner, maximum, contrast or equalization. Ten month identities, March–December, on twelve panels; both repeated April and repeated May count once in the primary distinct-month objective. Panel-count objective is secondary.

Collect every distinct cited month spelling in inspected historical sources whose dated witness lies c1350–1550 (inclusive 1350–1550 for admission). Record original form, month, language, region, regional variety, witness/composition date, source/edition date, precise locator, URL and evidence type. Do not replace a witness date with a modern dictionary's publication date or infer a region from a spelling. Unknown region/variety is explicitly unknown, not an invented attribution. Preserve these unresolved candidates outside the admitted scoring lexicon. An early composition qualifies only through a specifically dated in-window witness whose spelling is actually represented by the cited evidence. Undated historical dictionary lemmas, modern dialect forms and OCR reconstructions are candidates, not attested period spellings. Edited forms and direct diplomatic readings must be distinguished.

ALL means exhaustive extraction from the declared inspected source frame, with unresolved retrievals recorded; no finite audit can certify every spelling ever used across all regional varieties. No selection by target fit. Freeze source frame, complete candidate/admission ledger, admitted variants and SHA-256 manifest before any scoring. If adequate date/form provenance or complete ten-month coverage is unavailable in any primary language, keep source stage INCOMPLETE and do not calibrate an invented replacement. This source preflight is not a calibration FAIL or evidence of linguistic inseparability. January/February may be recorded bibliographically but are outside this ten-month test.

Source records sharing an identical normalized spelling in the same language/month form one variant, retaining every provenance record and variety tag. Counts report attestations and distinct scoring strings separately. No attestation-frequency weights or penalty for having more documented variants; the same maximum and null search freedom apply to each language. An unknown variety does not license a claim of all-variety coverage.

## Normalization, modes and cipher families

M1 uses Phase 9 normalization: NFD/diacritic removal, published special-letter replacements, lowercase letter-only strings, w→uu, j→i, k→c. Original strings remain in the ledger. Monoalphabetic uses one global injective mapping. Positional uses global injective beginning/interior/end mappings, first-character precedence for singletons. Naibbe uses the same six fixed published tables alpha, beta1, beta2, beta3, gamma1, gamma2, with exact dynamic-program search over 1/2-letter segmentations and no outer learned substitution. Tables are Voynich-informed controls, not blind cipher constructions. These are the Phase 9 bounded families, not all historical ciphers.

M2 adds these exact plaintext spelling alternatives: {o,u}; {i,j,y}; {lh,ll,ilh,li}; {nh,gn,ny,ni}; {c,ch,qu} immediately before a/o/u; {ss,s}; terminal {a,e,o}. Apply to plaintext name forms, never rename cipher symbols. Generate a finite one-pass transduction of each source spelling: at each original input offset enumerate every applicable declared class and literal copying, consuming the chosen original span; do not rescan or recursively expand emitted alternatives. Context tests refer to the original string, after basic Unicode letter cleaning and before Phase 9 j/k normalization. Final-vowel alternatives apply only to the original last character. The union with M1 retains the original spelling. No deletions, silent letters, arbitrary endings, anagrams or added phonetic classes. Every generated alternative records its derivation and is an equivalence-generated form, NOT an attested spelling. This finite rule prevents recursive lh/ilh expansion from creating an unbounded search.

Freeze EQUIVALENCES.json before source collation or scoring. Both modes receive identical maximization freedom on observed, synthetic and shuffled sets.

## Objectives, ties and primary inference

Fit a single family/mapping/table globally across panels for each language/mode. A month matches if at least one of its panels exactly matches an encoding of one of that month's allowed forms; duplicates cannot increase its primary weight. The primary score is the maximum number of distinct months matched (0–10), over both modes, all variant/annotation alternatives and families. Optimize panel count separately (0–12) for continuity, never substitute it for the primary objective. Preserve all language ties; a fixed display order is not identification. Calibration correct-language success requires a UNIQUE winner, with the generating language winning. A tie counts as failure.

Primary global test: shuffle whole annotation alternative bundles across the twelve fixed month-panel slots, 999 permutations. Each permutation refits every language/mode/family/variant and uses the same full primary maximum as observed. p=(1+number of null maxima >= observed maximum)/1000. The primary family includes all five languages and both modes; no per-language uncorrected p is reported as the global test. Secondary French uses its own separate maximum/null distribution and cannot rescue primary failure.

Occitan–Catalan contrast D = best distinct-month Occitan score minus best distinct-month Catalan score, each maximized over modes/families/variants. Upper-tail p_D=(1+#null differences >= observed difference)/1000 tests a directional Occitan advantage. Report signed observed D and the full null distribution; no reinterpretation of this contrast as a global language-identification p-value.

## Secondary prediction and budget equalization

LOMO removes EVERY panel of one month, fits on the other nine months, and predicts the held-out annotation without seeing its strings in fitting or tie-breaking. Keep all equally optimal training fits; only already observed mapping entries may predict (no held-out-driven completion of unmapped letters). For Naibbe use all tied training-optimal fixed tables and finite segmentations. Prediction sets and ambiguity are reported; no single favorable completion is selected. Count held-out months with an exact prediction, and separately with a Levenshtein distance <=1 prediction, across the frozen prediction set. Unmapped/empty predictions are failures, not dropped. Apply the complete procedure to each shuffled dataset and report secondary permutation tails. Output every month's training optimum, competing languages, predictions, exact/edit outcomes and unavailable mappings.

Budget equalization: for each month independently let k be the smallest number of distinct attested normalized variants among the five primary languages. In each of 100 fixed draws sample k distinct attested variants without replacement in every language/month, then derive its M2 alternatives and refit. Counts are equalized per month, not by discarding whole month identities. Report outright winner frequency and tie frequency separately; ties receive no arbitrary win credit. French has its own separately reported result, never changes the five-language budget. No equalization result promotes a failed primary/calibration result.

## Calibration before target access

Use twelve synthetic panels / ten month identities matching the known panel geometry. Within each language/month sample uniformly over frozen distinct attested variants (citations are not sampling weights); duplicate panels sample independently but share month identity and the global generating encoder. Five languages × eight encoding settings (mono, positional, six fixed Naibbe tables) × fifty replicates = 2000 positives; one independently shuffled wrong-pair negative per positive = 2000 negatives. All jobs, seeds, lexicons and shuffles must be frozen before generation. M2 spelling latitude is a scoring mode, not unrecorded noise generation.

Preserve the Phase 8 frozen ZL-profile rates and operation model as a copied, hashed dependency; do not estimate anything from the Phase 9b target. If its operation semantics cannot be reproduced, preflight remains incomplete. Use the Phase 9 author-derived Naibbe generator with its published settings, copied into this experiment. Log the known limitation that the historical ZL profile is native-script transcription disagreement, not measured noise in these Latin-script annotations. No no-noise rescue.

For EACH language × encoding-setting cell, at least 40/50 positives must uniquely identify the generating language under the full primary comparison. At most 2/50 wrong-pair negatives may reject at global alpha .05. Incomplete trials, source failures or search exceptions are not discarded or replaced. Every cell must pass; no aggregate override. Report Occitan/Catalan confusion matrices with five-language winners plus ties and other-language errors, by encoding setting and overall. If either cannot achieve 80% separation, STOP with the prescribed limited result: annotation set too short to resolve close Romance varieties under this registered method. Do not infer impossibility from a source/preflight failure, or invent a confusion matrix without running the bank. Stop at the first failed completed calibration cell; preserve all scheduled/unrun jobs and no rescue.

Seeds derive from SHA-256 of UTF-8 'phase9b-v1|language|encoder|replicate|purpose', first 8 bytes big-endian. Replicates 0–49. Shuffles and draws use separately keyed purpose fields; all concrete schedules and RNG/version bindings must be hashed before generation. No target fit until all calibration gates PASS.
