# Phase11b sources

Existing source bytes, licenses and pinned encoder/tokenizer provenance remain in ../phase11_plaintext_units_v1/SOURCES.md. Phase11b adds no corpus. C7 reclassification follows the actual existing C1 encoder and C7 metadata, not interpretation of resemblance.

- Michael A. Greshko, *The Naibbe cipher* (2025), https://doi.org/10.1080/01611194.2025.2566408 ; original implementation https://github.com/greshko/naibbe-cipher pinned719f2f4c4d1eacf95fabf48381cd84fc6cd94279. Use original author algorithm, standard respacing, accepted draw truth; no v2 substitution or oracle fitting. Non-blind Voynich-derived tables.
- Massimiliano Zattera, *A New Transliteration Alphabet Brings New Evidence of Word Structure and Multiple Languages in the Voynich Manuscript* (2022), https://ceur-ws.org/Vol-3313/paper10.pdf . Related alphabet/unit modeling, not a decipherment calibration result. T-Z is not an input to11b.
- Kevin Knight, Anish Nair, Nishit Rathod, Kenji Yamada (2006), *Unsupervised Analysis for Decipherment Problems*, COLING/ACL poster proceedings499–506, https://aclanthology.org/P06-2065/ . Noisy-channel EM decipherment; their simple-letter example estimates an English source-bigram model from news. This experiment instead learns its hidden transition matrix without language samples/priors.
- Sujith Ravi, Kevin Knight (2011), *Bayesian Inference for Zodiac and Other Homophonic Ciphers*, ACL239–247, https://aclanthology.org/P11-1025/ . Bayesian sparse-prior/type-sampling approach with letter n-gram language models and word dictionaries. Our single-site fully collapsed HMM sampler is related inference machinery, not a reproduction of that method or evidence of its documented accuracy without their language resources.
- Guannan Zhong (2016), *Cryptanalysis of Homophonic Substitution Cipher Using Hidden Markov Models*, SJSU Master's Projects505, DOI10.31979/etd.9x48-r4vp, https://scholarworks.sjsu.edu/etd_projects/505/ . HMM random-restart cryptanalysis and ciphertext-length dependence motivate the registered restarts and length curve. No numerical equivalence or language-free success is inferred from its abstract.
- Malte Nuhn, Julian Schamper, Hermann Ney (2014), *Improved Decipherment of Homophonic Ciphers*, EMNLP1764–1768, DOI10.3115/v1/D14-1184, https://aclanthology.org/D14-1184/ . Search-based homophonic decipherment with language-model scoring; not the freely learned HMM used here.

Primary publication pages/PDFs were checked2026-10-27. Numba numerical runtime is isolated under this extension, pinned0.63.1 with llvmlite0.46.0 and NumPy2.3.5; official compatibility reference https://numba.readthedocs.io/en/stable/user/installing.html . No system or JNSQ environment change. Runtime package code is software, not plaintext training material.
