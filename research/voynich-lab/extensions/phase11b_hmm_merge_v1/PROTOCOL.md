## HMM and two inference algorithms

Categorical-emission, first-order homogeneous HMM, K=18..32 inclusive, independently for each algorithm/tokenizer/replicate/length. Both transition and emission distributions are freely learned; no language model, language-specific bigram matrix, dictionary, known-frequency initializer, inventory target statistic or oracle constraint. Training observation vocabulary is its observed chunk types plus an explicit UNKNOWN emission; validation/evaluation unknown chunks map to UNKNOWN identically for all K and K=1 baseline. Report OOV counts. Shared fixed-tokenizer preprocessing is lossless; HMM emissions need not be one-to-one or deterministic.

EM (Baum–Welch):20 independently seeded random restarts per K, initial state/transition rows Dirichlet(1), emission rows Dirichlet(1/V). Scaled forward/backward on complete training sequences. Maximum100 iterations, minimum5, stop when absolute observed training-log-likelihood change per unit <=1e-6; all probabilities floored at1e-12 and renormalized. Record every iteration's observed likelihood, final iterations, endpoint flag, nonmonotonic/nonfinite flags and restart values. No language-derived transition initialization.

Collapsed Gibbs:20 independently seeded chains/restarts per K, random uniform initial state path. Integrate out initial, transition and categorical-emission parameters with symmetric Dirichlet total concentrations1 each (per-coordinate1/K or1/V). Exactly100 sequential single-site full sweeps; burn first50; inspect snapshots60,70,80,90,100. The conditional removes the site's emission and both adjacent edges, then uses the exact Dirichlet predictive with the shared-row/self-transition correction. For each retained path use posterior-mean parameters, rank retained snapshots by observed TRAINING likelihood. Preserve the chain likelihood trace including burn-in checkpoints; do not call these short chains proven mixed or converged. No EM polishing, label-dependent resampling, thinning/seed changes, best-truth selection, or stronger language prior.

For each algorithm/K, choose the greatest observed TRAINING likelihood among all20 restarts (and retained Gibbs snapshots); exact tie lowest restart, then earliest snapshot. Save that K's model and all restart summaries. Choose K by greatest VALIDATION observed log-likelihood only; exact tie smaller K. Do not choose on training LL across K, Viterbi likelihood, recovery, secondary band, A/B behavior, or true alphabet cardinality. EM and Gibbs remain separately reported branches; no after-the-fact algorithm switching per cell. Use Viterbi on FINAL evaluation sequences with selected frozen parameters. The categorical K=1 baseline is training-frequency MLE with the same probability floor and UNKNOWN vocabulary. Report validation and final-evaluation log-likelihoods, and final-evaluation gain over baseline in nats and nats/unit.

## Primary recovery gate

Report many-to-one accuracy and V-measure against synthetic true letters. Accuracy is state-cluster purity after optimal many-to-one evaluation mapping, denominator all true letter instances including fully deleted ones. This is an optimistic label-invariant recovery statistic, not a learned decryption key. V-measure is the harmonic mean of homogeneity and completeness on all instances, with missing instances assigned the shared missing-state cluster. No oracle labels choose fits, restarts or K. Every replicate reports selected K, true alphabet size, observed decoded inventory, final length, deletion/OOV coverage and both recovery statistics; no letter labels or correspondence tables are printed.

In each tokenizer x algorithm branch, require accuracy>=.80 AND V-measure>=.70 in >=16/20 replicates in EACH C1/C2 x52/78 cell. A branch qualifies at a length only if all four cells pass there. A full-length primary PASS means at least one fixed branch qualifies; preserve every other branch's failure. Report all three lengths even if full length fails. Short-length gate failures prohibit extrapolating the full-length result to A/B. Missing/numerically invalid fits, uncompleted K/restart sweeps or missing recovery cells are INCOMPLETE/NOT_ESTIMABLE, never negative evidence. Iteration caps are computational budgets, not proof of convergence; retain endpoint flags prominently.
