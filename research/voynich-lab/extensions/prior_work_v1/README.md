# Extending the model lab with published approaches and controls

> Plain-English reading guide to the archived snapshot. The linked reports contain the full rules, results, and qualifications. This guide is editorial material added after the original archive.

## What we tested

Collected pinned prior implementations, extended baselines, mixed-membership methods, dictionary comparisons, and additional neural work.

## What we found

The retained status records completed baselines and partial control qualification, including failed word/phrase recovery, while other work and final verification were still pending. Human coherence ratings remained blank.

## What remains unresolved

The status of one finished component does not establish completion of the full batch. No qualified latent-language or translation conclusion is supplied by the incomplete recovery work.
