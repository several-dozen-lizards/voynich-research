## Primary physical tests

Aggregate page posteriors to one leaf posterior by the arithmetic mean over available sides; leaf label is argmax, ties smallest component index. Report side agreement and uncertainty, do not suppress mixed/low-confidence leaves. Conjugacy statistic: proportion of complete observed bifolia whose two leaves share a label. Compare to1999 permutations (plus-one p), (a) shuffle whole-leaf labels within quire, (b) across all observed leaves. Recto/verso/panels remain attached to each leaf. Constant nulls are NULL_INVARIANT with no p, not evidence for association.

Quire coherence statistic: same-label fraction among all unordered within-quire leaf pairs, pooled by pair count. A shuffle within each quire preserves its label counts exactly and CANNOT test this statistic. Therefore “quire-preserving” means fixed quire slots/sizes: (a) permute intact leaf labels among those slots across the book, (b) permute intact physical bifolio blocks among sheet slots, stratified by observed block size, preserving conjugacy and missing-data structure. Report both; never present an invariant within-quire shuffle as a meaningful coherence p-value. This interpretation was explained to the user before registration.

Apply Holm across these four primary tests for every selected k within each transcription/seed (no p for invariant cases); report raw and adjusted p, null distributions and effects. Test each k selected anywhere across seeds/ZL/H in BOTH transcriptions. Do not pick a favorable k or seed. Physical labels are external annotations; clustering never sees them. Test membership associations with A/B, Davis hands, sections and quires using contingency tables, adjusted MI and both conditional entropies; mixed/unknown labels are explicit. Nesting inside A/B uses the full cluster x A/B table, H(AB|cluster), and effective cluster counts per A/B; do not force a2x2 story. Label-permutation-invariant ARI compares seed partitions and matched ZL/H page partitions, including when k differs. Report every selected k, its seed counts and complete likelihood curves.
