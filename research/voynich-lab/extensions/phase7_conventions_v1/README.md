# Can the method recover the number of writing conventions?

> Plain-English reading guide to the archived snapshot. The linked reports contain the full rules, results, and qualifications. This guide is editorial material added after the original archive.

## What we tested

Tested known one-, two-, and four-convention arrangements, including physical leaf and quire relationships, on constructed source material.

## What we found

None of the twenty-eight required cells passed the registered operating criteria. Selected group counts and physical tests did not reliably recover the known construction.

## What remains unresolved

The failure limits this instrument. It does not establish how many conventions the manuscript uses or identify a historical reading order.
