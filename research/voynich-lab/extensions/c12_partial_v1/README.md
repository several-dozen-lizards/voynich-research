# Decomposing only some shorthand signs into strokes

> Plain-English reading guide to the archived snapshot. The linked reports contain the full rules, results, and qualifications. This guide is editorial material added after the original archive.

## What we tested

Swept fixed partial-decomposition schemes while preserving reversibility and the original short-budget model instrument.

## What we found

None of the completed settings simultaneously matched all three frozen descriptive bands. A pre-metric endpoint bug was caught and preserved before the corrected runs.

## What remains unresolved

The bands are descriptive, and model fits may still be learning at their budget limit. No matching setting, reading, or historical mechanism was established.
