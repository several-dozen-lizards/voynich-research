# Phase 9 source-stage decisions

- The source frame is the complete published Zandbergen pre-1960 table, not a hand-picked set of plants chosen for token fit. Empty rows and unconfirmed alternatives are retained. This is not a claim that all published modern identifications have been collated.
- Preserve the six pre-existing v76 proposals separately. They include decipherment-associated identifications and unresolved primary-source chains; they cannot be counted as independent botanical corroboration. The f16r juniper/hemp/aconite disagreement and f41v coriander/fern/carrot alternatives must not disappear through an automatic name join.
- The user asked to try both zodiac approaches separately. Known Latin-script month annotations and putative native-script month cribs therefore have separate eligibility and future target gates. No native month ownership is inferred from a zodiac sign alone.
- The current identification table supplies folio-level claims, not independently documented native-script label loci. Do not invent paragraph-first plant labels or use a central star label as a month label. The absence of an admissible crib in this audit is not proof that no relevant label exists anywhere.
- Freeze existing name evidence and language gaps rather than fill the four-language matrix with modern translations, modern binomials or reconstructed spellings. Historical dictionary OCR is distinguished from a facsimile-verified period witness. No spelling corrections, abbreviation expansions or meaning-based truncations were performed.
- Direct raw-page downloads initially failed in the restricted shell. Public-source network retrieval obtained the 1909 Levy OCR; other sources returned HTTP 406, certificate or timeout errors. Those attempts are preserved. Web-tool extracts are saved and identified as extracts, not original HTML snapshots. Access failure is not evidence against a historical form.
- The source inventory is frozen before any scoring. The scoring protocol remains explicitly DRAFT/UNREGISTERED: a source freeze is not a calibration pass, an algorithm registration or a target-opening event. Do not mark calibration FAIL when it has not run.
- Phase 8's FAIL and its audit addendum remain unchanged. Its key-sharing and coverage diagnostics motivate explicit representation/null checks, but do not themselves establish a Phase 9 result or block an independently qualified future method.
