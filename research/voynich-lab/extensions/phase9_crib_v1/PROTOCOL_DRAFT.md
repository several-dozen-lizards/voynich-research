## Scoring specification to implement once source prerequisites are resolved

The requested primary statistic is the best label–name match achievable with **one global, consistent mapping per encoding model within each track**, maximizing over the declared encoding families and candidate languages. The fixed inventory, candidate alternatives and mapping constraints must be identical for real and shuffled data. No per-item remapping or post-outcome spelling additions.

- Plain monoalphabetic substitution: one injective letter mapping for all labels in the track. State the plaintext and ciphertext units explicitly; EVA code points and multigraphs cannot be interchanged after results.
- Naibbe-family verbose encoding: use the published Greshko tables and author normalization, with license/citation notices. Specify the finite fixed-table and/or mixed-table codebook models, allowed unigram/bigram segmentations, collision handling and treatment of spacing before registration. A stochastic published codebook is not automatically an injective letter substitution. No fitted glyph tables or undisclosed table-choice freedom.
- Positional-variant substitution: declare fixed position classes and one injective mapping per class shared across all labels. The position rule cannot be chosen label by label. Its additional flexibility must be included in every null optimization.

An exact-match count with a fixed eligible-item denominator is a concrete initial objective for implementation; any partial-match/edit-distance alternative must be settled before registration, not chosen by calibration results. A claimed best-achievable score requires an optimum certificate or an explicitly preserved search failure. A best-found approximate score is not a certified optimum. Report scorer/data-support failures, optimizer/search failures and failed scientific separation/recovery gates separately.

Nulls shuffle complete label bundles across illustrations **within section**, retaining a label's alternative readings and bundle structure. Refit/reoptimize the same global mapping and all declared choices for every shuffled dataset. Compute max-over-families on both the observed assignment and each shuffled assignment; language, spelling, segmentation and submodel searches must receive the same treatment. Report family scores as well as the corrected maximum. The null can include length/shape compatibility and is not uniquely a phonetic-mechanism test.

## Calibration before target access

Build synthetic manuscripts from frozen known-name lists with known encoding mappings under each declared family. Match the eventual eligible sample support, candidate ambiguity and label-bundle structure, rather than assuming a large dictionary solves a small-crib problem. Keep mapping truth separate from learner inputs. Require recovery of the true identifiable mapping on observed support and superiority to the same max-over-family shuffled null. Do not demand identification of unobserved alphabet entries or silently score an ambiguous mapping as uniquely recovered.

Declare positive-recovery and false-positive gates and seeds before outcomes. Include wrong-pair/independent-label controls using the complete selection procedure. Any family/support that is unidentifiable or fails qualification stays failed; no rescue by changing names, positional classes, alphabets, solver budget, score or null. Passing synthetic controls cannot establish the botanical identification or the ownership of a real label. Only the completed registered calibration gate could authorize its matching target track, after a frozen target manifest.
