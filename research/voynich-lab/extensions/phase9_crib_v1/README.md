# Checking whether proposed cribs have adequate sources

> Plain-English reading guide to the archived snapshot. The linked reports contain the full rules, results, and qualifications. This guide is editorial material added after the original archive.

## What we tested

Inventoried historical plant proposals, month annotations, period name forms, and four-language evidence coverage before registering scoring.

## What we found

The source-stage audit exposed missing prerequisites. The collected rows were proposals and evidence records, not a set of qualified plant-name pairs or a completed label/illustration experiment.

## What remains unresolved

Modern translations and scientific species names could not stand in for period spellings. Native target scoring and calibration remained closed at this checkpoint.
