## Review decisions that are methodological, not historical citations

The following follow from the measurement definitions/design and are explicitly amendments: a word-identity feature contains its terminal units; lack of a planted rule is not a guarantee of statistical independence; conditional randomization must state what conditioning is preserved; a non-significant test is not evidence of equivalence; a scalar JSD comparison does not uniquely identify a generative mechanism; subgroup significance cannot establish coverage of untested manuscript regions;36 labels do not enumerate all later mapping/search choices. These are not claims that the review has experimentally demonstrated those failures in JX. No JX calibration was generated or run.

Historical details not established here include specific regional month-name identifications, exact marginalia dates/hands, battlement localization, all36 proposed L/S/C co-attestations, quantitative phonetic effects for their period-specific orthographies, and the exact earliest provenance/date. The corrected drafts treat them as motivations or pending evidence, not frozen historical facts. The candidate list can be approved with NP operational predictions without converting this incomplete evidence audit into certainty.
