# A laboratory for comparing text structure and small predictive models

> Plain-English reading guide to the archived snapshot. The linked reports contain the full rules, results, and qualifications. This guide is editorial material added after the original archive.

## What we tested

Built source parsers, descriptive measures, constructed writing-system controls, and short-budget predictive models, then added later source, segmentation, and direction studies.

## What we found

The main Phase 0–5 pipeline completed its registered fits and engineering checks. Its models and structural comparisons provide reproducible instruments, with later extensions preserving both partial successes and failed qualification gates.

## What remains unresolved

The models were not trained to convergence or to translate. The early README stops at Phase 5; later extension and result folders have their own scopes and corrections, including important source-adapter failures.
