# Can a calibrated circular boundary be made unique?

> Plain-English reading guide to the archived snapshot. The linked reports contain the full rules, results, and qualifications. This guide is editorial material added after the original archive.

## What we tested

Tested unit mirroring, location of any retained boundary, and unique source origin in longer circular calibration examples.

## What we found

Mirroring and any-boundary recovery passed, but unique-origin support was absent. The run stopped before the remaining pairing-null stage.

## What remains unresolved

Finding some boundary does not identify a unique ring start. This did not repair the original radial failure or open real-ring scoring.
