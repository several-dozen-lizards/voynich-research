# Separate direction, origin, and pooled-test chains

> Plain-English reading guide to the archived snapshot. The linked reports contain the full rules, results, and qualifications. This guide is editorial material added after the original archive.

## What we tested

Ran the richer calibrated suite on paragraph-derived examples, controls, and source-sensitive views.

## What we found

Cross-boundary unit direction passed several ZL bins, but whole-word direction did not qualify there. Other chains had mixed failures, insufficient support, or missing-source stops.

## What remains unresolved

The signals are instrument- and representation-specific. A passing direction chain does not identify a ring start, seam count, plaintext space, or historical mechanism.
