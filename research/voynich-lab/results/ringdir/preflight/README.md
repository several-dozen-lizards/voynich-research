# Checking ring-direction identifiability before calibration

> Plain-English reading guide to the archived snapshot. The linked reports contain the full rules, results, and qualifications. This guide is editorial material added after the original archive.

## What we tested

Inventoried circular/radial source locations and tested authored counterexamples to the proposed direction and start rules.

## What we found

A maximum over rotations could not identify its own rotation, and equally strong boundaries need not identify one unique start. The initial proposal stopped for design review.

## What remains unresolved

The inventory counts source locations, not independently confirmed complete rings. No empirical calibration or corrected order existed at this stage.
