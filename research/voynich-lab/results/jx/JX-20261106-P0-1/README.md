# Junction-feature calibration results

> Plain-English reading guide to the archived snapshot. The linked reports contain the full rules, results, and qualifications. This guide is editorial material added after the original archive.

## What we tested

Executed the separately bound engineering, specificity, conditioning, spacing, and empirical-reference chains.

## What we found

Engineering and several control chains passed, but spacing-reference ordering failed. The reference profiles were descriptive only.

## What remains unresolved

Unit associations and reference-profile distances do not identify phonology, plaintext spaces, a language, or an accepted mapping. No interpretation-stage manuscript test ran.
