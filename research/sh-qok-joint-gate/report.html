Selection caveat

Feasibility depends on prefix orientations and structural follower availability. It need not stay invariant under the conditional null. Therefore the null checks are an essential part of this joint gate; outcome-blind selection alone does not guarantee unbiased inference. Passing these finite controls is not a mathematical proof of validity across arbitrary arrangements.

Within each accepted layout, two nulls and a positive reuse the same matched structure with independently drawn outcomes. They are paired scenarios, not independent replication. Four-body controls are more diverse than one-body controls but remain authored synthetic arrays, not a historical corpus. Exact lengths, variable-length parsing, H/F alignment and actual manuscript support are not qualified by this run.
