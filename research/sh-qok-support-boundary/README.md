# Checking sensitivity just above the proposed support cutoff

> Plain-English reading guide to the archived snapshot. The linked reports contain the full rules, results, and qualifications. This guide is editorial material added after the original archive.

## What we tested

Tested new aligned and fragmented constructed layouts close to the minimum usable-support boundary, with page-concentration limits.

## What we found

The declared nuisance and planted-positive gates passed for the specified arrangements and effect sizes. Rejected layouts and all attempted seeds remained in the record.

## What remains unresolved

This limited boundary qualification is not a universal guarantee, an independently chosen minimum, or evidence that real manuscript panels meet the rule.

Compressed `.gz`/`.xz` files retain the original bytes; the repository restoration helper can unpack them. HTML reports may require a local browser rather than GitHub’s file viewer.
