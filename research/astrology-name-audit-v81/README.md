# Are there enough securely identified zodiac names?

> Plain-English reading guide to the archived snapshot. The linked reports contain the full rules, results, and qualifications. This guide is editorial material added after the original archive.

## What we tested

Inventoried surviving zodiac panels, their labels, alternate readings, and historical written sign names.

## What we found

Twelve panels supplied ten sign identities, but not ten securely identified Voynich name tokens. Transcription gaps and a disagreement over scribal attribution were retained.

## What remains unresolved

The link from pictured sign to written word is missing. A name-decoding benchmark with supplied name pairs cannot be transferred to these uncertain labels.
