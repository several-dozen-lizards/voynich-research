Page settings and local association coexist descriptively

The fraction sh among all initial-sh/ch tokens varies across pages: H bath37.5–59.0%, F bath36.6–60.9%; H starred11.5–56.8%, F starred11.6–58.3%. A context-conditioned spelling choice can correlate with a following word. So can collocation, grammatical adjacency, a graphic/junction convention, copying or notation. Association does not establish that choosing sh changes the next word, or identify either unit as a thing or an action. The updated model is page preference plus a local conditional association, with mechanism unresolved.
