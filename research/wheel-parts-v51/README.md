# Locating text inside, on, and outside diagrams

> Plain-English reading guide to the archived snapshot. The linked reports contain the full rules, results, and qualifications. This guide is editorial material added after the original archive.

## What we tested

Mapped source-described diagram units to spatial categories and inspected their word-part profiles, retaining ambiguities and missing rings.

## What we found

The inventory made the spatial labels explicit, but comparable same-format contrasts were too sparse for the desired spatial interpretation. One reading lacked ring coverage, and parts of the Rosettes inventory were missing.

## What remains unresolved

This was not fresh blinded image registration. Labels and ring text cannot simply be pooled as if format and location were interchangeable.
