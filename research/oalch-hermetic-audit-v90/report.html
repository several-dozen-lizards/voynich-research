The medieval bridge is real; the plant name needs care

Gower’s late-fourteenth-century Confessio Amantis VII.1320–1327 explicitly associates Clota/Pliades with crystal and fennel. This is a pre-Voynich witness for the correspondence, without relying on a modern table. In VII.1310–1318 Aldeboran instead has Carbunculum and Anabulla. A historical medical text connects Anabulla/titimaillus with spurge. The later English Agrippa has “Milky Thistle”; equating that wording to the modern species Silybum marianum would require another taxonomic step. Retain both traditions rather than silently imposing one botanical identity.

No fennel illustration has been independently selected in this audit, so no fennel-page association is claimed. The cluster as Pleiades remains a conditional identification; it does not transfer to the connector or Aries figure label.
