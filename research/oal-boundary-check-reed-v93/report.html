What follows for the prediction

The astronomy-exclusive oal marker prediction is contradicted by its exact and initial occurrences elsewhere. A boundary-insensitive oal+ch pattern is also not exclusive. This weakens the proposed general marker explanation; it does not prove the three joined oalch tokens happened by chance. A restricted combination may be meaningful even when both components are broad. It also does not prove alch is never a unit: no single segmentation has been established.

No Pleiades/lunar-mansion identification is advanced from these counts. Such a prediction needs a specified diagram referent, ordered locations or other independently assessable correspondence. It cannot be rescued or rejected solely by choosing whichever substring distribution looks best.
