# Inspecting plant details and dated name forms

> Plain-English reading guide to the archived snapshot. The linked reports contain the full rules, results, and qualifications. This guide is editorial material added after the original archive.

## What we tested

Looked at full scans and close details for the castor and pansy candidates, then checked headings in a dated historical herbal.

## What we found

The candidates remained plausible but provisional; key diagnostic features were not demonstrated and visible mismatches remained. The dated headings supplied conditional written names from one later source tradition.

## What remains unresolved

The names do not establish earlier local usage, species identification, or the meaning of the paragraph opener. No sound values were fitted.
