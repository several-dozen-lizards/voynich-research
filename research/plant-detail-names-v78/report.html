Palma Christi · 1485

Gart der Gesundheit, Mainz — chapter 310; BSB bsb00032739 image 498

Verification: Printed heading and descriptive passage inspected directly in enlarged facsimile; BSB bibliographic record gives Mentz 1485.

Spelling: Palma Christi is an expanded reading of the abbreviated printed heading; not a diplomatic character string. No pronunciation supplied.

Fit: Historical castor candidate: palmately divided leaves in illustration and text comparing leaves to vine leaves. Historical depiction is stylized and does not independently establish f6v identity.

Ambiguity: Palma Christi is not an exclusive botanical identifier across all traditions. This witness does not establish use in the Voynich region or before 1485.

Iacea / freyschem krut · 1485

Gart der Gesundheit, Mainz — chapter 432; BSB bsb00032739 image 674

Verification: Heading and adjacent description inspected directly in enlarged facsimile; same dated BSB volume.

Spelling: Reader transcription with long-s normalized. Iacea/Jacea is an orthographic reading alternative, not two independent names. Word division freyschem krut retained; exact diplomatic spelling requires specialist review.

Fit: Text describes three flower colours (yellow, blue, white); woodcut has divided leaf-like structures and violet-like flowers. Supports pansy context, not modern taxonomic precision.

Ambiguity: Iacea/Jacea is not universally exclusive to pansy. Botanical context must accompany this form. Freyschem nomenclature has a therapeutic context; no inference that every similarly named plant is Viola tricolor.
