# Which zodiac labels are actually comparable?

> Plain-English reading guide to the archived snapshot. The linked reports contain the full rules, results, and qualifications. This guide is editorial material added after the original archive.

## What we tested

Inspected paired Aries and Taurus images and compared the written forms associated with their labels and rings.

## What we found

Some unusual spelling candidates survived comparison across readings, but no isolated Voynich word was established as the sign's name. The later readable month annotations were kept separate.

## What remains unresolved

A label near an animal can refer to something else. Image placement and spelling ranks do not establish a name, a pronunciation, or a decipherment.
