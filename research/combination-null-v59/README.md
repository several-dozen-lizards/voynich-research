# Are recurring word combinations more than frequency effects?

> Plain-English reading guide to the archived snapshot. The linked reports contain the full rules, results, and qualifications. This guide is editorial material added after the original archive.

## What we tested

Compared recurring pairs and triples with shuffled versions of the same lines, preserving word counts, boundaries, and important layout conditions.

## What we found

None of the 276 paired patterns passed the corrected test in both main readings. The stricter correction and the earlier result were both retained.

## What remains unresolved

The test was conservative and weak for rare combinations. This does not show that the manuscript lacks phrases or ordinary language, and it supplies no meanings.
