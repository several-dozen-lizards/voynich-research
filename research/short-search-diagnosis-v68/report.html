Diagnosis

All nine old controls now exceed 90% held-out letter recovery under the revised search. In particular Latin case2 rises from11.9% to94.1% without extra text. This demonstrates recoverability of the old failures, not guaranteed recovery by the revised method.

Fresh revised20-word consecutive cases pass Latin3/3, MiddleEnglish2/3, German2/3. Same20 words shuffled pass3/3,3/3,2/3. All revised remaining test-letter errors occur on letters absent from the training plaintext: MiddleEnglish i/k, German l/w. Missing-symbol assignments are underdetermined by the training sample. The German best fit also out-scores the true training plaintext, showing that language-model score is not a guarantee of the exact key.

Both methods recover100% of held-out letters in all nine100-word cases. This is finite engineering evidence on fresh positions in the same works, not independent new works or genres. The20- and100-word comparisons share test passages; arms are not independent replications. More words add both context and symbol coverage. The shuffled20 arm does not establish that word order is useless.

No revised manuscript search was run. The useful future safeguard is to leave assignments for unseen cipher symbols unresolved instead of rendering arbitrary optimizer values as readings. That change is proposed, not qualified here. Semantic coherence and syllabic/rebus systems remain untested.
