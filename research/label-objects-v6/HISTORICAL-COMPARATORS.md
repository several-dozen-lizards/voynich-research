# Multilingual period comparators — 24 October 2026

The user's follow-up requested documents in multiple languages from approximately
the same period and region. The search prioritised fifteenth-century European
medical and herbal manuscripts. These are comparison leads, not evidence for a
Voynich language, place of origin, plant identity or decoding.

* **Penn LJS 419, Erbario:** northern Italy, fifteenth century. The owning
  institution describes medicinal annotations in Italian, Latin and mixed
  language. This is the closest regional lead in this search, conditional on
  an Italian comparison region; that region is not established for Voynich.
  [Penn manuscript inventory](https://openn.library.upenn.edu/html/0001.html),
  [curator's description](https://schoenberginstitute.wordpress.com/2024/03/25/manuscript-monday-ljs-419-herbal-video-orientation/).
* **Glasgow Hunter 251:** England, c.1475–1500. The owning library describes
  Latin medical writing with English and Anglo-Norman material. Useful for
  keeping alternative herb names and language changes separate; it is a
  later and geographically different comparison.
  [Glasgow description and manuscript images](https://www.gla.ac.uk/myglasgow/library/files/special/exhibns/month/may2006.html).
* **Wellcome MS.MSL.136:** mid-fifteenth century. The catalogue identifies a
  Latin botanical glossary on ff.96r–97v with English names in the margins,
  alongside English remedies. This provides a concrete layout comparison
  for glosses versus prose, not a set of independently verified plant images.
  [Owning-library catalogue](https://wellcomecollection.org/works/gjvuqvfp).
