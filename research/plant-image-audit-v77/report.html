f16r — Juniper remains disputed; no species anchor

Inspected manuscript image · H: pocheody / F: pocheody

Hand 1; Currier A; Botanical; paragraph text. H/F are alternate readings, not replication.
Visible features
Broad star-like green structures on stalks, terminal clusters of small reddish marks, thick divided root.
Supports
Reddish fruit-like marks and pointed green forms are the broad features behind the juniper proposal.
Mismatch / unresolved
The large radiating leaf-like structures do not clearly show the needle leaves in whorls of three expected for oxycedrus. Red marks alone are not diagnostic.
Original-source audit
Bax 2014 is the original retrieved juniper proposal; Peterson’s response was prompted. Aconite/hemp antecedent notebook passages not directly inspected in this audit.
Historical-name compatibility
Fructus iuniperi: berries, not a bare plant name. CELT glossary associates juniper with communis; it cannot verify oxycedrus. No trimming to iuniperi or inferred pronunciation.
Retained alternatives
Juniperus oxycedrus; Juniperus, species unresolved; Aconitum (ELV attribution in compilation); Cannabis / hemp (Petersen attribution in compilation); unidentified / composite
