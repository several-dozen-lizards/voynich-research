What survived?

The strongest descriptive result is right-neighbour breadth in hand-3 starred paragraphs: daiin exceeds 33/36 frequency-band peers in H and 20/20 in F after sampling 20 events per word. These are descriptive ranks, not independent replication or corrected significance. Expected distinct types are 19.50 (H) and 19.47 (F) out of 20: the metric is near its ceiling.

Hand-2 botanical daiin is near the middle (H 40th percentile on each side; F 56th left / 50th right). Baths are moderately broad (H 70th left / 54th right; F 79th / 61st). Hand-1 botanical and recipe strata have only one or two matched peers. Other formats lack enough daiin data; those failures remain in BREADTH.json. A manuscript-wide glue-word claim is unsupported by this comparison.

The positive candidate-content prediction fails in all 12 eligible H/F tests, even before multiplicity correction (smallest one-sided p=.194). Recipe slopes are +.050 H and +.074 F candidate words per additional daiin at matched page/length/uncertainty; neither is distinguishable from its conditional null. This does not establish an exact zero effect. Candidate contents remain the fixed chedy, shedy, chey, cheol set, not identified materials. Unknown slots stay visible. Exact ordinal and paragraph position are not additional controls in this line-level test.

Phonetic / rebus hypothesis

The supplied anomaly percentages and examples are not independently verified here. Irregular words need not be phonetic, and phonetic words need not be irregular. Recurrent unusual combinations can also arise from morphology, abbreviations, scribal conventions or systematic transcription choices. Removing a first sign may reveal a prefix or decoration, but a residual that remains rare does not establish sound-spelling. Labels and paragraph openings are positions, not independently identified name slots.

A useful next experiment would learn a template on training folios, freeze its anomaly score, compare held-out positions with word length/frequency/hand/format controls, and track whether the same unusual groups survive H/F segmentation alternatives. Test initial-sign removal against deletion elsewhere and matched ordinary words; use a held-out ordinary-word vocabulary. A positive result would establish a local spelling regime. A phonetic interpretation needs independently anchored name readings or successful predictions of unseen names.
