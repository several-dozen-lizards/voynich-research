Interpretation limits

A positive screen is conditional dependence beyond a specified nuisance predictor, not proof that position, spelling, phrases or latent registers are exhausted. Constant fallback can meet the adequacy rule while still omitting meaningful local structure. The t procedure is approximate and relies on independent testing folios and score calibration. Actual event counts approximate the synthetic sizes but not their four-body vocabulary or uniform layouts. H/F are alternative readings, not independent manuscripts. Grammar and variable-slot templates may generate identical text. A negative screen cannot exclude smaller or differently represented dependencies.
