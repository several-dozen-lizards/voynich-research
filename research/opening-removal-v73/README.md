# Do opening words become ordinary words when their first sign is removed?

> Plain-English reading guide to the archived snapshot. The linked reports contain the full rules, results, and qualifications. This guide is editorial material added after the original archive.

## What we tested

Compared initial and final sign removal at source-marked paragraph openings with matched ordinary positions and separately learned vocabularies.

## What we found

No comparison passed both readings. Some spelling matches existed, but several tests had too few pages to attain the required corrected significance level at all.

## What remains unresolved

That attainable-limit problem prevents a decisive negative conclusion. Written-form membership also cannot by itself identify an opening sign's function.
