## What was verified

Pingree’s printed Excerpt VI directly supplies all 28 mansion names from Ambrosiana M.28.sup., ff. 92–92v. Its dating and complete passage are on PDF pp 51–53 (printed li–liii). The images were checked against extraction: Almiceni, Aldirach, Azimech, Algafra, Alichil, Nachaim, Zaadazohoc and Algarf alnichar replace OCR confusions. Mobra and Aliniquecdea really are printed that way and remain unchanged. Position 27 repeats the printed ordinal XXVI; numerical sequence position and source ordinal are separate fields. The edition’s I.4 main text remains a separate spelling profile. Its admission is based on the securely early tradition, not an assertion that each eclectic spelling individually occurs before 1440.

Boudet’s critical-edition heading and manuscript descriptions (pp 851–852,871) date both A and B before 1440. The selected list passages and apparatus were checked as page images (pp 876–877,882–884). Main-list and invocation spellings differ within the same text; these are not harmonized. The planetary catalogue has groups of 4, 4, 4, 4, 3, 3, 4 names, totalling 26. The invocation prints 25, with repetitions and without the catalogue’s Datquiel. Neither supplies 24 hour names or 28 mansion spirits. B spelling substitutions are applied together, as a complete apparatus-derived profile. For the seven seal names, the explicitly enumerated second profile uses B’s Mychael from the repeated-name reference on p 877. It is labelled a permitted spelling profile, not a diplomatic copy of the p 876 main list.

The actual Reg.lat.1300 f. 45v image confirms the seven-name planetary sequence and planet assignments. Grévin and Véronèse (2004), p 331 n. 84, report the manuscript’s revised date as the second half of the14 th century, correcting the older c1500 date. The fixed spellings are Peterson’s edition-linked transcription: Capciel, Saquiel, Samael, Raphael, Anael, Michael, Gabriel. The handwritten second name was not independently resolved letter by letter; Saquiel is attributed to that editorial transcription, not presented as a new palaeographic finding. Other Raziel books and witnesses inherit neither this date nor these spellings automatically.

## Variant and morphology rules

LISTS.json contains complete permitted profiles and a position-by-position variant registry. No Cartesian mosaic of different witnesses is allowed. All nine profiles must enter every relevant null search; with two, three or four surviving arms this is18,27 or36 list/profile–arm pairs, before rotations, directions and band orders. Equal-complexity random keys and the four C5 nulls must include the complete applicable search. No names may be inserted, gaps repaired, profiles truncated or padded, or source units relabelled to fit geometry. A further source or spelling requires a new declared freeze; it cannot be added during alignment after seeing results.

Suffix fields use the longest literal ending in the specified inventory (-iel/-ael/-uel/-yel/-el). They are orthographic candidates, not individual etymological proofs. Spelling ihel is not silently rewritten as iel. Mansion names are not assumed theophoric; their C-STEM form stays intact. Initial Al in Latinized mansion names is recorded as a surface prefix candidate. Anael is not assigned an Arabic article. C3 must distinguish list units and cannot turn25 invocation positions into 24 hours, or26 planet-group members into 28 mansions.

## Exclusions and limits

SOURCE-AUDIT.json closesfourteenth centuryandidate-family decisions. “Excluded” is an admission decision for this freeze, not proof that the tradition has no medieval list. This freeze is bounded by the selected sources; it does not certify an exhaustive manuscript collation.

- Picatrix IV.9 mansion and spirit lists remain in EXCLUDED-DRAFT-LISTS.json. Both retain the printed gap at22; Selehe/Siely remains recorded. The separate microfiche apparatus was not acquired, and early passage coverage of the spirit list was not established.
- Ars Notoria has an early witness: Yale Mellon MS 1, c1225, according to its owning library. Its discipline-specific notae and prayer names were not established as an ordered astral-unit list. Approximately36 drawings do not establish36 decans.
- The Alfonsine Lapidary’s h-I-15 witness is securely c1270–1275 per the owning library. Its manifest was acquired. The360 degree-stone and36 stone spellings and variants were not fully collated; modernized editions cannot certify manuscript orthography. Those lists are excluded.
- Greek, Latin and Arabic decan-name editions and complete variants were not verified sufficiently for admission. No composite modern table was substituted.
- Sachau’s Arabic-mansion translation was acquired and all 28 station headings visually inspected (pp 343–350). Its modern romanization, diacritics and aliases were not fully collated into an admissible variant set. It remains excluded; Latin forms are not substituted for Arabic spellings.
- Heptameron day/hour arrangements remain in LISTS-LATE.json. The inspected edition is based on1559, with a1508 reference to substantially matching text; broadly15 th-century precursor dates do not establish the complete arrangement by 1440. Agrippa1533, Trithemius1508 and the later Lemegeton are retained as late catalogue decisions, with no reconstructed early claim lists.

No 12-sign, 24-hour, 30-per-sign, 36-decan, 72-quinance or 360-degree claim profile is admitted. Any later negative result must say “for this frozen source set”; it cannot exclude ordered-name hypotheses in general. Geometry compatibility and the full alignment/null implementation remain C3 work.
