This is a source preparation supplement after C2 look 6. It does not amend `C1-source-freeze-v1/LISTS.json`, admit another variant or supply a C3 key.

The Lapidary's medieval witness date meets the date threshold. It does not date each modern spelling. The saved 260-page modernized PDF is an export of Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes and is a locator only. Its contents suggest highly incomplete Leo/Pisces blocks; this has not been turned into a numerical lacuna reconstruction. All 360 worksheet rows are blank. No statement that 360 distinct named stones survive is made.

The opening manuscript images have enough legibility to identify the first stone rubric and the degree-one passage; no full diplomatic transcription has been certified. Four folios do not establish the remaining book. The source may offer named stones, descriptive phrases and aliases in different languages. Those types need separate annotation and fixed treatment before a list is released.

The political roster has a genuine source-defined cardinality of thirty. It is not a chronological sequence of thirty appointments or a degree list. Modern English names and modern Greek editions are not automatically manuscript-specific spelling profiles. Pre-1440 manuscript transmission is supported by the edition notice, but the target passage needs direct witness or apparatus verification. The pre-1440 status must not be transferred from a Book I papyrus to this Book II passage.

The BOE 2021 Lapidary edition was also located. Earlier bibliographic leads are not sufficient to certify its page-by-page relation to a nineteenth-century facsimile or modernized transcription. No BOE text was downloaded or admitted here; catalogue metadata and other reproductions must be checked against its title and contents before using that edition as a spelling authority. Prior frozen exclusions remain unchanged.
