# Checking whether uniform clothing labels matched the images

> Plain-English reading guide to the archived snapshot. The linked reports contain the full rules, results, and qualifications. This guide is editorial material added after the original archive.

## What we tested

Reinspected the exposed development images without consulting alignment fits, preserving the original labels and all corrections.

## What we found

Five eligible groups contained both clothed and bare figures after correction. The earlier zero-variation calculation was correct for its stored labels but could not stand as a claim that the drawings themselves lacked variation.

## What remains unresolved

This was a same-agent reinspection, not independent blinded annotation. It supplied corrected observations, not a new alignment result or winning rule.
