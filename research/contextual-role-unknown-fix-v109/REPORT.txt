OUTCOME
The fixed repair improves the original supervised scorer on the exposed development comparisons, while making its unresolved decisions explicit. It does not solve broad contextual-role recovery or qualify a blind role-discovery method. No full supported comparison reaches80% all-case balanced correct-credit.

Across the four prespecified adequately supported panels, equal-panel mean balanced correct-credit rises69.02% ->72.52%, +3.50 percentage points. The descriptive paired whole-block bootstrap95% interval for the mean delta is[+0.80,+6.39] points. This measure assigns0.5 correct-credit to a tie: it is expected correctness under equal random tie-breaking, NOT an actual successful classification of an abstained case.

The repaired scorer abstains on71 of648 test instances in the four supported panels, pooled coverage89.04%. Abstentions are missing evidence, not recovered roles. Report this tradeoff with any improvement claim.

WHAT CHANGED
Only globally unseen-feature handling. The original score sums six terms log((class_count(feature)+1)/(class_total+training_vocabulary_size+1)). The repaired score sums the SAME terms only for features present anywhere in the pooled binary-role training vocabulary. A feature unseen in BOTH roles contributes zero to both scores. A feature absent in only one role remains evidence with unchanged add-one smoothing. The class totals, vocabulary, +1 denominator, counts, equal priors and known-feature weights are unchanged. Score differences within1e-12 are explicit abstentions with0.5/0.5 normalized score probabilities. No-evidence cases have exact0/0 scores.

WHAT STAYED FIXED
The four surface neighbors, exact positions and two exact pair features; vocabulary learning rule; original whole-block train/test split; same eight panels and role definitions; source labels; example eligibility and all saved baseline predictions. No backoff, wider window, character features, external embedding, lemma, POS, morphology or dependency feature was added. Clustering and automatic role-count selection were not altered or rerun. This repairs a SUPERVISED diagnostic scorer; it does not repair the unsupervised k-means representation by itself. Original v107/v108 code, failures and results remain unchanged.

All3,184 declared control trials completed:199 within-block independent context-column shuffles and199 within-block training-label shuffles per panel, each refitting the same scorer. Test labels never train the repaired model. These controls retain real source/block marginals and may preserve substantial structure, so their means need not equal50%.
