# Is exact-position matching enough to remove nuisance effects?

> Plain-English reading guide to the archived snapshot. The linked reports contain the full rules, results, and qualifications. This guide is editorial material added after the original archive.

## What we tested

Qualified a conditional comparison matching page, line length, exact position, and word body on constructed cases.

## What we found

Position control helped, but a shared line-level setting still produced misleading signals and a required page-control bound also failed. The method remained unqualified.

## What remains unresolved

The experiment identified an omitted line-level nuisance, rather than a manuscript mechanism. No real text was rescored.
