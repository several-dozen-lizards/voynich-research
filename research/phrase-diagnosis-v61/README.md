# Why was the phrase detector missing known phrases?

> Plain-English reading guide to the archived snapshot. The linked reports contain the full rules, results, and qualifications. This guide is editorial material added after the original archive.

## What we tested

Diagnosed sample-size, layout, and correction choices on known prose, then froze a smaller phrase-selection procedure before separate samples.

## What we found

The narrower procedure recovered several ordinary phrases and flagged none of twenty shams, meeting its limited gate. Other chosen phrases and an apostrophe artifact still failed and were retained.

## What remains unresolved

This was a narrow later-herbal check with artificial lines. It did not establish medieval transfer, broad semantic qualification, or a new manuscript result.
