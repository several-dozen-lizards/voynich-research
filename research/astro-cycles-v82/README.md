# Do twelve-part diagrams behave like named cycles?

> Plain-English reading guide to the archived snapshot. The linked reports contain the full rules, results, and qualifications. This guide is editorial material added after the original archive.

## What we tested

Compared the order of written tokens around a twelve-part diagram with historical naming traditions, allowing rotations and reversals.

## What we found

The exact-order matches did not stand out from shuffled sector orders. Historical name forms were collected, but no diagram was identified as a sequence of signs, houses, or months.

## What remains unresolved

The reading order and the connection between a sector and a particular name remain unestablished. No sound values were fitted.
