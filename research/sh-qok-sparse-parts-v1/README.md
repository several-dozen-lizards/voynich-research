# Developing an interaction test for sparse parts

> Plain-English reading guide to the archived snapshot. The linked reports contain the full rules, results, and qualifications. This guide is editorial material added after the original archive.

## What we tested

Prepared covariates, matching profiles, identifiability checks, and a frozen constructed-data qualification schedule for the sparse-context interaction study.

## What we found

This snapshot contains per-trial records and qualification progress, including a recorded interruption and resume. It does not contain a completed owning results report that establishes an overall scientific pass.

## What remains unresolved

Progress records and passing individual cells are not a completed qualification. No manuscript conclusion should be inferred from the existence of the runner or its saved trials.
