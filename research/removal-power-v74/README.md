# Could the opening-removal test detect an effect if present?

> Plain-English reading guide to the archived snapshot. The linked reports contain the full rules, results, and qualifications. This guide is editorial material added after the original archive.

## What we tested

Planted known prefix effects on layouts matching the earlier deletion experiment and checked false alarms and detection rates.

## What we found

False alarms were low in the specified controls, but detection missed the required sensitivity targets, including for strong effects. The manuscript was not rescored.

## What remains unresolved

The earlier lack of a passing deletion result is therefore not a decisive absence finding. These constructed lexicons do not cover every possible language mechanism.
