# Preparing source and generator comparisons for writing-system hypotheses

> Plain-English reading guide to the archived snapshot. The linked reports contain the full rules, results, and qualifications. This guide is editorial material added after the original archive.

## What we tested

Built known-source corpora, constructed writing-system controls, coverage checks, and staged hypothesis definitions before fresh-reserve testing.

## What we found

The later development source cut resolved its recorded size and literal-unit coverage gaps, and arithmetic/engineering checks passed within their scope. The claim definitions and H3 remained unresolved.

## What remains unresolved

Development qualification did not open fresh reserves or establish full recovery, model survival, or a manuscript fit. Register, source, and glyph-coverage differences remained explicit.
