Limits that remain

These model classes do not exhaust position, junction spelling, copying, or phrases. Prediction of a supplied prefix from surrounding text is conditional analysis, not left-to-right text generation. Local-model failure can mean sparse vocabulary, missing interactions, or regularization error. Longer templates, latent registers, and context-sensitive orthography can mimic an extended relation. A negative extended-context result cannot establish absence of grammar, particularly where power or novel-frame coverage is weak. No reserved zodiac material was opened.
