Handling verified

Unknown origins and unknown immediate followers are excluded; no gaps or line ends are crossed.

Comma-adjacent uncertain tokens remain null; exclamation marks are removed as in the original atlas; brace comments become uncertainty barriers except the atlas's trailing-comment cleanup. Dedicated fixtures cover these cases. Positions are original zero-based indices, never ranks among readable or eligible tokens. This verifies consistency with the saved transcription policy, not the correctness of every manuscript reading.

Critical scope limit

The synthetic qualification used eight fixed origin positions: zero-based1,3,5,7,9,11,13,15. This audit retains those positions and does not search alternatives. Many manuscript lines do not reach the later positions, and many eligible origins fall outside them. Those exclusions are counted separately in SUMMARY.json.

Matching is within physical page and exact line length. The variable-length implementation partitions without pooling, but the earlier synthetic controls used fixed-length arrays. This audit is therefore a support check for the specified adaptation, not end-to-end validation of a manuscript test.

Hundreds of eligible origins do not imply movable comparisons: the chosen position pairs also require the same exact body in both positions, and discordant sh/ch rows in both orientations within a page/length group. No selected group supplies positive V. This demonstrates a limitation of this exact fixed-position method; it does not show that every legal rearrangement or other defensible test is unavailable.

The earlier bath association is neither rescored nor overturned. There is no new positive or negative association result. Any different position scheme, wider matching or pooling rule would be a new method requiring fresh qualification.
