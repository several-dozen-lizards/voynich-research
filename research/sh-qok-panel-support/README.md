# Does the manuscript supply enough legal exact matches?

> Plain-English reading guide to the archived snapshot. The linked reports contain the full rules, results, and qualifications. This guide is editorial material added after the original archive.

## What we tested

Audited eligible lines and readable following slots under the frozen exact matching rule without calculating qok outcomes.

## What we found

All four target panels had zero informative matching support under this rule. Many readable words therefore did not translate into usable controlled comparisons.

## What remains unresolved

Unsupported is not a negative association result. No outcome classification, effect score, or p-value was calculated.
