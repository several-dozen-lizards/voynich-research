# Explaining the exact-word frame search's negative discovery

> Plain-English reading guide to the archived snapshot. The linked reports contain the full rules, results, and qualifications. This guide is editorial material added after the original archive.

## What we tested

Checked which frozen criteria eliminated the complete universe of screened early frames.

## What we found

Every manuscript frame failed both the required paragraph presence and the repeated-variant criterion. The known Latin positives behaved differently under these same rules.

## What remains unresolved

The diagnosis did not retune the method or promote near misses. It explains this registered result rather than ruling out all formula-like text.
