# Applying the qualified spelling-class frame search

> Plain-English reading guide to the archived snapshot. The linked reports contain the full rules, results, and qualifications. This guide is editorial material added after the original archive.

## What we tested

Used the fixed spelling-class engine on discovery paragraphs separately for two scribes.

## What we found

Neither discovery panel supplied an eligible frame. Both null selections were frozen, and all four confirmation cells remained untested.

## What remains unresolved

No candidate means there was nothing eligible to transfer under these rules. It is not a general rejection of formulas, grammar, or every possible encoding.
