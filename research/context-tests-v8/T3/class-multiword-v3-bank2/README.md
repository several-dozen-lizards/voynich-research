# A fresh isolated replacement control bank

> Plain-English reading guide to the archived snapshot. The linked reports contain the full rules, results, and qualifications. This guide is editorial material added after the original archive.

## What we tested

Retested the unchanged class-based method on a separately registered bank with isolated workers.

## What we found

All 1,200 trials were valid and none produced a full passing control result; the reused Latin positive cells also passed. The method received limited control qualification on this bank.

## What remains unresolved

The earlier failed bank is preserved separately. One exposed Latin source and fixed constructed mechanisms do not establish broad positive power on manuscript-scale material.
