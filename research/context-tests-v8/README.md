# Testing links between text, pictures, and recurring frames

> Plain-English reading guide to the archived snapshot. The linked reports contain the full rules, results, and qualifications. This guide is editorial material added after the original archive.

## What we tested

Organized separate studies of image/text relationships and recurring text frames, with rules intended to prevent discovery choices from leaking into confirmation.

## What we found

The initial report records several blocked or incomplete arms. Later source-control work includes a narrowly qualified replacement control bank, but the class-based manuscript discovery found no eligible candidate to confirm.

## What remains unresolved

This folder has a long sequence of amendments and follow-ups; its initial report is not a complete current summary. Independent image judgments, source support, and unopened confirmation remain distinct requirements.
