# Can a predictor separate local writing from longer context?

> Plain-English reading guide to the archived snapshot. The linked reports contain the full rules, results, and qualifications. This guide is editorial material added after the original archive.

## What we tested

Compared bounded local predictors with extended-context alternatives on constructed mechanisms before fitting manuscript data.

## What we found

The first attempt failed its required adequacy or power gate and did not proceed to manuscript fitting. An explicit equal-output control showed that a grammar-like rule and a phrase template could look identical to the test.

## What remains unresolved

Even an extended-context gain would only reject the specified limited comparator. It could not uniquely establish grammar.
