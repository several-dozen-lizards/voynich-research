THE ONE FROZEN SUPPORT FORMULA
For each real context word at position i:
  n_local = pooled training occurrence count of i:word
  n_pool = pooled training occurrence count of W:word at all four offsets
  n_other = n_pool - n_local
  w_local = n_local/n_pool; w_shared = n_other/n_pool, when n_pool>0
  both weights0 when total support is absent
Each recognized word occurrence has a unit total weight. Its evidence is w_local*log_likelihood(i:word) + w_shared*log_likelihood(W:word). The support counts are pooled over roles; the weight calculation never inspects heldout labels, test frequencies, or which role a feature favors. Local counts are not included a second time in n_other.

The shared likelihood itself still pools all four offsets, including local occurrences; its weight alone uses the disjoint other-position count. The channels remain statistically overlapping, and three other positions provide more count opportunities than one local position. Support is a feature-event count, not an independently sampled effective size or proven estimate of predictive reliability. These limitations were declared before scores. No opportunity correction, alpha, strength threshold or fitted weight was added after this result.

Boundary markers use positional evidence only, at weight1 if recognized and0 otherwise. Original two pair features retain their original contributions, including singleton pairs. Repeated words in separate slots preserve separate unit budgets. A feature absent in one class but present globally keeps ordinary add-one smoothing; globally unknown terms remain neutral. Completely unsupported contexts abstain at equal scores.

DECISION AND IMPLICATION
Retain v109 as the conservative reference and preserve v113 as a failed count-weighting candidate. Do not treat raw recurrence counts as established evidence of reliability for this task. This result does not reject every support-weighting or interpolation scheme: it rejects adoption of this one untuned ratio on these outcomes.
A further combiner would need a new design that measures training-only predictive reliability, with a separate independent confirmation source sealed before selecting a rule. Neither the equal-mixture control nor another count formula was adopted or tuned after seeing these results. Other branches' sealed reserves remain untouched.
