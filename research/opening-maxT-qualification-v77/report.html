Frozen method

Each signed count contrast is centred by its pooled observed+shuffle mean and divided by √(pooled variance + 1 count²). Each row’s largest absolute standardized contrast across the entire original opening family supplies the null maximum. The target is compared directly with those maxima, instead of separately ranking thousands of tiny tail probabilities. No normal-distribution tail approximation is used.

The one-count regularizer is fixed at the integer count scale and keeps rare or constant contrasts from producing unbounded standardization. It changes the relative weighting of the search family; this is an explicitly different multiplicity statistic. Every original candidate is retained. Mean and scale are symmetric across rows, preserving the finite exchangeable-row ranking argument.

All 14 original H/F hand/section/Currier/rawP panels and the Bonferroni factor14 are unchanged. No hands/readings are pooled, and no extra manuscript shuffles or narrower candidate search are introduced.
