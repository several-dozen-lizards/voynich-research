INTERPRETATION
The prose family resemblance is supported descriptively: both f/p favor opening lines, with p stronger at the initial word. The proposed ring-segment-start explanation has no observed structural boundary at slot9 and no boundary change across its four copies. This does not exclude a nonlocal, unmarked or procedural relationship; none is demonstrated here. No meanings, sounds, interchangeable-sign equivalence, causal mechanism or out-of-sample cross-format prediction follows.
