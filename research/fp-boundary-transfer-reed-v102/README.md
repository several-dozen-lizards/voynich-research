# Can prose opening signs explain a ring's boundary?

> Plain-English reading guide to the archived snapshot. The linked reports contain the full rules, results, and qualifications. This guide is editorial material added after the original archive.

## What we tested

Compared prose f/p opening preferences with the actual image geometry of the disputed slot on the f57v diagram.

## What we found

The prose signs share an opening preference, but the ring slot is internal to its transcribed run and lacks the proposed immediate divider. Geometry from other rings could not supply its missing boundary.

## What remains unresolved

The proposed direct boundary transfer was unsupported. Sparse exact matches also prevented a validated f-specific cross-format rule.
