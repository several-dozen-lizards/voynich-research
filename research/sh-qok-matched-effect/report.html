Positive feasibility labels apply to the proposed positive even on null rows. No support means no group can change orientation. Infeasible means the available matched followers cannot attain the target rate without probabilities exceeding1. Neither is a negative association result. These failures count against practical power; feasible-only counts are also saved in SUMMARY.json.

Nulls use independent probability.2 on nonorigin followers, without prefix-conditioned calibration. Their finite-layout matched expectation may differ slightly because available followers differ between prefixes. Positive calibration is deliberately a power construction and must not be mistaken for a null model.

The target is the weighted aggregate across matched groups, not a15-point effect separately within every group. The exact test statistic remains total sh*qok, so this is not a claim of equivalent conditional alternatives for every layout. Realized effects fluctuate around their calibrated expectation, especially with tiny support.

Each positive gate requires a Wilson95 lower detection bound≥.80 across all100 attempted layouts. Null upper bound must be≤.10. All failures remain visible; powerless nulls cannot establish useful calibration. No thresholds changed after outcomes.

One-body controls are deliberately favorable. Twelve-body controls and variable lengths are authored sparse scenarios, not fitted manuscript distributions. Different design cells use fresh layouts rather than identical paired body alternatives. Within each layout, null and positive share matching. No corpus parser or H/F alignment qualification is claimed.
