# Counting support that can actually affect the outcome

> Plain-English reading guide to the archived snapshot. The linked reports contain the full rules, results, and qualifications. This guide is editorial material added after the original archive.

## What we tested

Defined a support measure that gives no credit to fully blocked followers, then tested it on new constructed layouts.

## What we found

The declared controls and planted fifteen-point differences passed under the supported architectures. Separate checks showed that adding blocked cases could not artificially improve the support measure.

## What remains unresolved

The pass is limited to this simulation scope and effect size. It does not establish support or an effect in every manuscript context.
